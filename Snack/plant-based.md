## Air Fryer Potato Chips with Onion Dip

**Serves:** 2 · **Time:** 30 min (hands-on 10) · **Equipment:** air fryer, mandoline or sharp knife · **Est. cost:** $2.20 total

| Per serving | Amount | % of 1,600 day | % of 2,000 day |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Calories | 237 kcal | 15% | 12% |
| Protein | 19 g | 16% | 13% |
| Carbs | 35 g | 22% | 18% |
| Fat | 2 g | 4% | 3% |
| Fiber | 4 g | 11% | 11% |
| Sodium | 355 mg | 15% | 15% |

**Storage:** 🔥 Make fresh — chips are same-day (the dip is 🥡 fridge prep, 4 days)

**Why it's filling:** a real bowl of chips (potatoes are one of the most satiating foods there are) plus a protein-packed "sour cream and onion" dip.

### Ingredients
- 2 small russet potatoes (~9 oz total), skin on
- 1 tsp olive oil (or spray)
- ⅛ tsp salt, plus pepper/garlic powder/smoked paprika to taste
- **Dip:** 1½ cups nonfat plain Greek yogurt + 1 tsp dry onion soup or ranch mix + chopped chives/green onion
- 1 cup baby carrots (extra dippers)

### Instructions
1. Slice the potatoes paper-thin (1/16 inch). A mandoline makes this easy.
2. Soak the slices in cold water 10 min to remove starch, then dry them *very* well with towels.
3. Toss with oil and salt. Air fry at 360°F for 12–16 min in a single loose layer (2–3 batches), shaking every 4 min. Pull them out as they turn golden, since thin ones finish first.
4. Stir the dip together while the chips cook.

### Notes
- **Crisp tip:** chips firm up as they cool. Let them sit 2 min before judging.
- **Storage:** best the same day. The dip keeps 4 days.

## Crispy Air Fryer Chickpeas with Tzatziki

**Serves:** 6 (½ cup chickpeas + ½ cup tzatziki each) · **Time:** 25 min (hands-on 10) · **Equipment:** air fryer · **Est. cost:** $6.00 total

| Per serving | Amount | % of 1,600 day | % of 2,000 day |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Calories | 196 kcal | 12% | 10% |
| Protein | 16 g | 13% | 11% |
| Carbs | 23 g | 14% | 12% |
| Fat | 4 g | 8% | 6% |
| Fiber | 5 g | 13% | 13% |
| Sodium | 330 mg | 14% | 14% |

**Storage:** Pantry — 3 days in a loosely covered container (the tzatziki is 🥡 fridge prep, 4 days)

**Why it's filling:** crunchy, salty chickpeas replace chips, and the fiber (5 g) and yogurt protein make the snack last.

### Ingredients
- 2 cans (15 oz) chickpeas, drained, rinsed and patted very dry
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1 tsp smoked paprika, ½ tsp garlic powder, ½ tsp cumin, ¼ tsp salt
- **Tzatziki:** 3 cups nonfat plain Greek yogurt, 1 cup grated cucumber (squeezed dry), juice of 1 lemon, 2 cloves garlic (grated), 1 tbsp dried dill, scant ¼ tsp salt

### Instructions
1. Air fry the dry chickpeas plain at 390°F for 10 min, shaking halfway.
2. Toss with oil and spices, then air fry 6–10 more min until crunchy.
3. Mix the tzatziki ingredients. Portion into 6 containers.
4. Cool the chickpeas completely before storing, or they go soft.

### Notes
- **Eat:** dip the chickpeas into the tzatziki, or eat them by the handful with tzatziki on the side.
- **Flavor swaps:** curry powder and garam masala; everything seasoning; togarashi (Japanese chili blend).
- **Storage:** chickpeas stay crunchy for up to 3 days in a loosely covered container (they soften if airtight). Re-crisp in the air fryer 2 min. The tzatziki keeps 4 days.

## Spicy Garlic Edamame

**Serves:** 2 · **Time:** 10 min (hands-on 5) · **Equipment:** microwave or rice cooker steaming basket, skillet · **Est. cost:** $4.90 total

| Per serving | Amount | % of 1,600 day | % of 2,000 day |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Calories | 235 kcal | 15% | 12% |
| Protein | 20 g | 17% | 13% |
| Carbs | 15 g | 9% | 8% |
| Fat | 12 g | 23% | 18% |
| Fiber | 8 g | 21% | 21% |
| Sodium | 315 mg | 14% | 14% |

**Storage:** 🥡 Fridge prep — 3 days

**Why it's filling:** a big bowl of edamame is a complete plant protein with 8 g of fiber. Buy them in the pod and popping each bean out slows you down, like at an izakaya.

### Ingredients
- 12 oz frozen shelled edamame (about 2½ cups), or a 24–32 oz bag in the pod
- 1 tsp toasted sesame oil
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 2 tsp low-sodium soy sauce
- 1 tsp chili crisp (or sriracha/sambal)
- Pinch of flaky salt

### Instructions
1. Steam the edamame: microwave per the bag (~5 min) or steam in the rice cooker basket for 8–10 min.
2. In a skillet, warm the sesame oil and garlic over medium for 1 min until fragrant, then stir in the soy and chili crisp.
3. Toss the edamame in the sauce and sprinkle with flaky salt.

### Notes
- Nutrition is for 12 oz of shelled beans. In-pod bags vary a lot by brand in how much bean you actually get, so check your label if you go the pod route; the pods do slow you down, izakaya-style.
- **Lazy version:** microwave, then toss with chili crisp only.
- **Storage:** 3 days in the fridge. Good cold, too.
