## Microwave Edamame

**Serves:** 2 · **Time:** 5 min (hands-on 1) · **Equipment:** microwave · **Est. cost:** ~$2

| Per serving | Amount | % of 1,600 day | % of 2,000 day |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Calories | 188 kcal | 12% | 9% |
| Protein | 18 g | 15% | 12% |
| Carbs | 14 g | 9% | 7% |
| Fat | 8 g | 15% | 12% |
| Fiber | 8 g | 21% | 21% |
| Sodium | 160 mg | 7% | 7% |

**Storage:** 🧊 Freezer — steam-in-bag edamame keeps months frozen.

**Why it's filling:** A full cup of edamame is 18 g of protein and 8 g of fiber, and you eat it slowly.

### Ingredients (for two)
- 1 steam-in-bag frozen shelled edamame (about 2 cups)
- Salt (or everything bagel seasoning)

### Instructions
1. Microwave the bag per the package (about 4 min).
2. Split into two bowls, salt, eat.
