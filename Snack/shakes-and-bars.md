## Fairlife Protein Shake + Clementine

**Serves:** 2 · **Time:** 1 min (hands-on 1) · **Equipment:** none · **Est. cost:** ~$4

| Per serving | Amount | % of 1,600 day | % of 2,000 day |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Calories | 185 kcal | 12% | 9% |
| Protein | 31 g | 26% | 20% |
| Carbs | 13 g | 8% | 6% |
| Fat | 2.6 g | 5% | 4% |
| Fiber | 2.3 g | 6% | 6% |
| Sodium | 271 mg | 12% | 12% |

**Storage:** 🥫 Pantry — shelf-stable until opened; tastes best cold from the fridge.

**Why it's filling:** 30 g of protein in a bottle is the biggest protein hit for the calories.

### Ingredients (for two)
- 2 Fairlife Nutrition Plan shakes (11.5 oz, Costco)
- 2 clementines

### Instructions
1. Open, drink, peel, eat.

### Notes
- Protein runs above the 15–25 g snack target, which is fine for weight loss.
