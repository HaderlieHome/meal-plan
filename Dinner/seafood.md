# Seafood Dinners

Fish and shrimp dinners, mostly Japanese and Thai-leaning. Aim for fish twice a week for omega-3s.

**Seafood is cook-fresh only — never batched or stored as leftovers.** Every recipe here serves 2 and is eaten the night it's made. Only seafood-free parts (plain rice, slaw, sauces without fish) are ever prepped ahead.

- [Air Fryer Teriyaki Salmon Rice Bowls](#air-fryer-teriyaki-salmon-rice-bowls): 560 kcal · 53 g protein
- [Thai Red Curry Shrimp](#thai-red-curry-shrimp): 550 kcal · 45 g protein
- [Miso Ramen with Shrimp](#miso-ramen-with-shrimp): 460 kcal · 49 g protein
- [Spicy Tuna Rice Bowls](#spicy-tuna-rice-bowls): 510 kcal · 40 g protein
- [Air Fryer Cod Fish Tacos with Cabbage Slaw](#air-fryer-cod-fish-tacos-with-cabbage-slaw): 530 kcal · 44 g protein

> **Shrimp sodium:** many frozen shrimp are treated with a salt/phosphate solution (up to 4–5x the sodium). Buy bags whose ingredients say just "shrimp". Nutrition below assumes untreated shrimp.

## Air Fryer Teriyaki Salmon Rice Bowls

**Serves:** 2 · **Time:** 25 min (hands-on 10) · **Equipment:** Air Fryer, Rice Cooker · **Est. cost:** ~$16 total

| Per serving | Amount | % of 1,600 day | % of 2,000 day |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Calories | 560 kcal | 35% | 28% |
| Protein | 53 g | 44% | 35% |
| Carbs | 56 g | 35% | 28% |
| Fat | 14 g | 26% | 21% |
| Fiber | 10 g | 26% | 26% |
| Sodium | 830 mg | 36% | 36% |

**Storage:** 🔥 Cook fresh

**Why it's filling:** a poke-style bowl piled with cucumber, cabbage, carrot and edamame over 50/50 rice and cauliflower rice. It's colorful, crunchy and big.

### Ingredients
- 12 oz wild sockeye salmon (Costco frozen fillets), 2 portions, thawed
- **Teriyaki glaze:** 2 Tbsp low-sodium soy sauce, 1 Tbsp honey, 1 tsp cornstarch, ½ tsp grated ginger, 1 clove garlic
- ⅓ cup dry Japanese short-grain rice + 2 cups frozen cauliflower rice
- 1 cup shelled edamame (frozen)
- 1 cucumber (sliced), ½ cup shredded carrot, 1 cup shredded purple cabbage
- 1 tsp sesame seeds, 2 green onions (sliced)

### Instructions
1. Start the rice in the rice cooker. Put the edamame in the steamer basket for the last 8 minutes.
2. Whisk the glaze and **microwave for 30–45 sec** until thick.
3. Brush the salmon with half the glaze. **Air fry at 400°F for 8–10 min** (skin-side down if it has skin) until it flakes and reaches 145°F. Brush with the rest of the glaze.
4. Microwave the cauliflower rice and stir it into the rice. Build the bowls: rice, salmon, edamame, veg, sesame and green onions.

### Notes
- Swap: Atlantic salmon has more omega-3s but about 115 kcal and 12 g fat more per serving.
- Add-ons: sriracha yogurt drizzle (see the Spicy Tuna bowls), kimchi for gut health.
- Storage: eat the salmon the same day; don't keep leftovers. Seafood-free prep: the rice and the chopped cucumber, carrot and cabbage keep 3–4 days in the fridge.

## Thai Red Curry Shrimp

**Serves:** 2 · **Time:** 25 min (hands-on 15) · **Equipment:** Instant Pot on Sauté (or skillet), Rice Cooker · **Est. cost:** ~$16 total

| Per serving | Amount | % of 1,600 day | % of 2,000 day |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Calories | 550 kcal | 34% | 28% |
| Protein | 45 g | 38% | 30% |
| Carbs | 55 g | 34% | 28% |
| Fat | 17 g | 32% | 25% |
| Fiber | 9 g | 24% | 24% |
| Sodium | 1,190 mg | 52% | 52% |

**Storage:** 🔥 Cook fresh

**Why it's filling:** light coconut milk keeps it creamy for half the fat, and a full bag of broccoli plus peppers bulks out every bowl.

### Ingredients
- 12 oz raw peeled shrimp (thawed)
- 1 can (13.5 oz) light coconut milk
- 2 Tbsp Thai red curry paste (Thai Kitchen or Maesri)
- 1 tsp fish sauce + 1 tsp brown sugar
- 1 red bell pepper (sliced), 1 onion (sliced)
- 12 oz broccoli florets
- ⅓ cup dry jasmine rice + 2 cups frozen cauliflower rice
- 1 Tbsp lime juice. Basil or cilantro to finish

### Instructions
1. Start the jasmine rice in the rice cooker.
2. Instant Pot on **Sauté** (or a skillet): stir the curry paste with ¼ cup of the coconut milk for 1 minute until fragrant.
3. Add the rest of the coconut milk, fish sauce, brown sugar, onion, pepper and broccoli. Simmer 5–6 minutes until the veg is crisp-tender.
4. Add the shrimp and simmer 3 minutes, just until pink. Stir in the lime juice.
5. Stir the microwaved cauliflower rice into the rice and ladle the curry over it.

### Notes
- Swap: chicken breast chunks (simmer 8 min) or tofu.
- Heat: curry paste brands vary a lot. Start with 1 Tbsp if you're not sure.
- Storage: eat it the same day; don't keep leftovers. Seafood-free prep: plain rice keeps 4 days in the fridge.

## Miso Ramen with Shrimp

**Serves:** 2 · **Time:** 20 min (hands-on 15) · **Equipment:** Instant Pot on Sauté (or pot) · **Est. cost:** ~$14 total

| Per serving | Amount | % of 1,600 day | % of 2,000 day |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Calories | 460 kcal | 29% | 23% |
| Protein | 49 g | 41% | 33% |
| Carbs | 54 g | 34% | 27% |
| Fat | 6 g | 11% | 9% |
| Fiber | 10 g | 26% | 26% |
| Sodium | 1,940 mg | 84% | 84% |

**Storage:** 🔥 Cook fresh

**Why it's filling:** half real ramen and half konjac noodles gives huge bowls of slurpable noodles and hot broth at under 500 kcal.

### Ingredients
- 12 oz raw peeled shrimp
- 4 cups low-sodium chicken broth
- 2½ Tbsp white miso paste
- 1 tsp toasted sesame oil, 2 cloves garlic (grated), 1 tsp grated ginger
- 3 oz dried ramen noodles (plain, not instant packets) + 1 bag (7 oz) konjac/shirataki noodles, rinsed well
- 2 heads baby bok choy (halved), 4 oz mushrooms (sliced)
- ¾ cup frozen corn (Sapporo-style), 2 green onions
- Optional: chili crisp, nori strips

### Instructions
1. Instant Pot on **Sauté** with the sesame oil: cook the garlic and ginger 30 seconds. Add the broth and bring to a simmer.
2. Add the mushrooms and bok choy and simmer 3 minutes. Add the shrimp and corn and simmer 3 more minutes.
3. Meanwhile, boil the ramen per the package (3–4 minutes) and toss the konjac noodles in for the last minute. Drain and split between 2 bowls.
4. Turn off the heat. Whisk the miso with a ladle of broth, then stir it into the pot (boiling miso dulls it). Ladle over the noodles and top with green onions.

### Notes
- Traditionally served with a soft-boiled egg. It's skipped per the egg rule.
- Sodium runs high (about 1,940 mg, 84% of the daily limit), mostly from the miso (about 1,080 mg per bowl). Use 1½ Tbsp miso to cut about 430 mg per bowl, and keep the rest of the day's meals low-sodium.
- Storage: eat it the same day; don't keep leftovers.

## Spicy Tuna Rice Bowls

**Serves:** 2 · **Time:** 20 min (hands-on 10) · **Equipment:** Rice Cooker · **Est. cost:** ~$11 total

| Per serving | Amount | % of 1,600 day | % of 2,000 day |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Calories | 510 kcal | 32% | 26% |
| Protein | 40 g | 33% | 27% |
| Carbs | 59 g | 37% | 30% |
| Fat | 13 g | 25% | 19% |
| Fiber | 10 g | 26% | 26% |
| Sodium | 850 mg | 37% | 37% |

**Storage:** 🔥 Cook fresh (open the can the day you eat it)

**Why it's filling:** the spicy-tuna-roll experience in bowl form, with cucumber, edamame and avocado. No cooking beyond the rice.

### Ingredients
- 2 cans (5 oz) chunk light tuna in water, well drained
- **Spicy "mayo":** ⅓ cup nonfat Greek yogurt + 1 Tbsp light mayo + 1 Tbsp sriracha
- ½ cup dry sushi rice + 1 Tbsp rice vinegar + 1 tsp sugar
- 1 cup shelled edamame (frozen)
- 1 cucumber (diced), ½ cup shredded carrot, ½ avocado (sliced)
- 1 pack (5 g) roasted seaweed snacks, crumbled
- 2 green onions, 1 Tbsp low-sodium soy sauce

### Instructions
1. Cook the rice in the rice cooker with the edamame in the steamer basket for the last 8 minutes. Stir the vinegar and sugar into the hot rice.
2. Mix the tuna with the spicy mayo.
3. Bowl: rice, spicy tuna, cucumber, carrot, edamame and avocado. Top with seaweed, green onions and a drizzle of soy.

### Notes
- Mayo contains a little egg as a minor ingredient, which is fine per the egg rule.
- Stick with **light** (skipjack) tuna rather than albacore for lower mercury, 2–3 times a week max.
- Storage: open the can and mix the tuna the day you eat it; don't keep leftover tuna mix. Seafood-free prep: the seasoned rice keeps 4 days in the fridge (microwave it with a splash of water). Slice the avocado fresh.

## Air Fryer Cod Fish Tacos with Cabbage Slaw

**Serves:** 2 (3 tacos each) · **Time:** 25 min (hands-on 15) · **Equipment:** Air Fryer · **Est. cost:** ~$14 total

| Per serving | Amount | % of 1,600 day | % of 2,000 day |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Calories | 530 kcal | 33% | 26% |
| Protein | 44 g | 37% | 29% |
| Carbs | 58 g | 36% | 29% |
| Fat | 16 g | 30% | 24% |
| Fiber | 14 g | 37% | 37% |
| Sodium | 810 mg | 35% | 35% |

**Storage:** 🔥 Cook fresh

**Why it's filling:** 6 loaded tacos for two, with a crunchy lime slaw, pico and avocado. Air-fried fish instead of beer-battered saves hundreds of calories.

### Ingredients
- 12 oz cod fillets (Costco frozen), thawed and patted dry
- **Seasoning:** 1 tsp chili powder, ½ tsp cumin, ½ tsp garlic powder, ¼ tsp salt, 1 tsp olive oil
- 6 corn tortillas (6")
- **Slaw:** 3 cups coleslaw mix + ¼ cup nonfat Greek yogurt + 1 Tbsp lime juice
- **Crema:** ⅓ cup nonfat Greek yogurt + 2 tsp sriracha (or chipotle adobo) + 1 Tbsp lime juice
- ½ cup pico de gallo or salsa
- 1 avocado, sliced

### Instructions
1. Rub the cod with the oil and seasoning. **Air fry at 400°F for 8–10 min** until it flakes and reaches 145°F.
2. Toss the slaw ingredients together. Stir the crema.
3. Warm the tortillas: wrap in a damp paper towel and microwave 30 seconds, or **air fry at 350°F for 1–2 min**.
4. Build: tortilla, flaked cod, slaw, pico, avocado and crema.

### Notes
- Swap: shrimp (air fry 6–8 min) or tilapia.
- Want it crispier? Dust the fish in 2 Tbsp cornstarch before the oil (+30 kcal/serving).
- Storage: cook the fish fresh and eat it the same day; don't keep leftover fish. Seafood-free prep: the slaw (dress it the day you eat it) and crema keep 3 days.
