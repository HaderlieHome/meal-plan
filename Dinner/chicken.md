## Crock Pot Salsa Chicken Bowls

**Serves:** 4 (2 meals for two) · **Time:** 4–6 hr on Low (hands-on 5) · **Equipment:** Crock Pot, microwave · **Est. cost:** ~$16 total

| Per serving | Amount | % of 1,600 day | % of 2,000 day |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Calories | 531 kcal | 33% | 27% |
| Protein | 46 g | 38% | 31% |
| Carbs | 60 g | 38% | 30% |
| Fat | 11 g | 21% | 16% |
| Fiber | 6 g | 16% | 16% |
| Sodium | 1,480 mg | 64% | 64% |

**Storage:** 🧊 Freezer-friendly — the chicken keeps 4 days in the fridge or 3 months frozen.

**Why it's filling:** A big pile of shredded chicken over rice with salsa and cheese.

### Ingredients
- 1½ lb boneless skinless chicken breasts
- 1 jar (16 oz) salsa
- 1 packet taco seasoning
- 2 pouches (8.5 oz) microwave rice
- ½ cup shredded Mexican cheese

### Instructions
1. Put the chicken in the crock pot. Sprinkle on the taco seasoning and pour the salsa over it.
2. Cook on Low 4–6 hours.
3. Shred the chicken with two forks right in the pot.
4. Microwave the rice pouches.
5. Scoop rice into bowls, top with chicken and a sprinkle of cheese.

### Notes
- Tortillas work too: roll it into tacos or burritos.
- Leftovers make an easy lunch.

**Storage:** 🧊 Freezer-friendly — the BBQ chicken keeps 4 days in the fridge or 3 months frozen. Buy buns and slaw fresh.

**Why it's filling:** A heaping BBQ chicken sandwich with a side of coleslaw.

### Ingredients
- 1½ lb boneless skinless chicken breasts
- ¾ cup bottled BBQ sauce (e.g. Sweet Baby Ray's)

### Notes

## Air Fryer Chicken Thighs & Fries

**Serves:** 2 · **Time:** 35 min (hands-on 5) · **Equipment:** Air Fryer · **Est. cost:** ~$7 total

| Per serving | Amount | % of 1,600 day | % of 2,000 day |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Calories | 543 kcal | 34% | 27% |
| Protein | 41 g | 34% | 27% |
| Carbs | 43 g | 27% | 22% |
| Fat | 24 g | 45% | 36% |
| Fiber | 3 g | 8% | 8% |
| Sodium | 1,094 mg | 48% | 48% |

**Storage:** 🔥 Cook fresh — fries go soggy as leftovers.

**Why it's filling:** Juicy chicken thighs and a real plate of fries.

### Ingredients
- 14 oz boneless skinless chicken thighs
- 10 oz frozen french fries (about ⅓ of a bag)
- ½ tsp seasoned salt (e.g. Lawry's)
- Cooking spray
- Ketchup

### Instructions
1. Spray the thighs and sprinkle with seasoned salt.
2. Air fry at 380°F for 18–20 min, flipping once, until 175°F inside.
3. Take the chicken out and let it rest.
4. Air fry the fries at 400°F for 12–15 min, shaking once.
5. Serve with ketchup.

### Notes
- The 5 qt basket fits the chicken or the fries, not both, so cook them one after the other.

## Rotisserie Chicken Night

**Serves:** 2 · **Time:** 15 min (hands-on 5) · **Equipment:** Microwave · **Est. cost:** ~$9 total

| Per serving | Amount | % of 1,600 day | % of 2,000 day |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Calories | 514 kcal | 32% | 26% |
| Protein | 46 g | 38% | 31% |
| Carbs | 51 g | 32% | 26% |
| Fat | 16 g | 30% | 24% |
| Fiber | 8 g | 21% | 21% |
| Sodium | 712 mg | 31% | 31% |

**Storage:** 🥡 Fridge prep — leftover chicken keeps 3–4 days.

**Why it's filling:** A store rotisserie chicken, a big baked potato and broccoli. Zero cooking.

### Ingredients
- 1 store rotisserie chicken (you'll use about 10 oz of meat)
- 2 large russet potatoes
- 4 tbsp sour cream
- 1 bag (12 oz) steam-in-bag broccoli
- Salt, pepper, butter to taste

### Instructions
1. Poke the potatoes with a fork and microwave 8–10 min, turning once, until soft.
2. Microwave the broccoli bag.
3. Pull the chicken off the bone (skip the skin).
4. Plate the chicken, split the potatoes and top with sour cream, and add the broccoli.

### Notes
- The leftover chicken is lunch tomorrow (Caesar wrap or chicken sandwich).
- Butter isn't in the nutrition numbers. A pat adds about 35 kcal.

## Instant Pot Japanese Chicken Curry

**Serves:** 4 (2 meals for two) · **Time:** 40 min (hands-on 10) · **Equipment:** Instant Pot, Rice Cooker · **Est. cost:** ~$13 total

| Per serving | Amount | % of 1,600 day | % of 2,000 day |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Calories | 599 kcal | 37% | 30% |
| Protein | 38 g | 32% | 25% |
| Carbs | 65 g | 41% | 32% |
| Fat | 20 g | 38% | 30% |
| Fiber | 4 g | 11% | 11% |
| Sodium | 1,154 mg | 50% | 50% |

**Storage:** 🧊 Freezer-friendly — the curry keeps 4 days in the fridge or 3 months frozen. Make rice fresh.

**Why it's filling:** Thick, rich curry with chicken and potatoes over a bowl of rice.

### Ingredients
- 1½ lb boneless skinless chicken thighs, cut into chunks
- 1 box (3.2 oz) S&B Golden Curry sauce mix
- 1 large potato, cut into chunks
- 1 cup baby carrots
- 1 cup frozen diced onion
- 3 cups water
- 1 cup white rice

### Instructions
1. Start the rice in the rice cooker.
2. Put the chicken, potato, carrots, onion and water in the Instant Pot.
3. Pressure cook on High for 8 min, then let it release on its own for 5 min and vent.
4. Switch to Keep Warm, break in the curry blocks and stir until it thickens (about 3 min).
5. Serve over rice.

### Notes
- Mild, medium or hot all work. It's the same recipe on the back of the box.

## Air Fryer General Tso's Chicken

**Serves:** 2 · **Time:** 25 min (hands-on 10) · **Equipment:** Air Fryer, microwave · **Est. cost:** ~$9 total

| Per serving | Amount | % of 1,600 day | % of 2,000 day |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Calories | 543 kcal | 34% | 27% |
| Protein | 48 g | 40% | 32% |
| Carbs | 74 g | 46% | 37% |
| Fat | 7 g | 13% | 10% |
| Fiber | 6 g | 16% | 16% |
| Sodium | 438 mg | 19% | 19% |

**Storage:** 🔥 Cook fresh — it's best crispy. Leftovers keep 3 days but go soft.

**Why it's filling:** Sweet, sticky takeout-style chicken with rice and broccoli, no deep frying.

### Ingredients
- 12 oz boneless skinless chicken breast, cut into bite-size chunks
- ⅓ cup bottled General Tso's sauce
- 1 pouch (8.5 oz) microwave rice
- 1 bag (12 oz) steam-in-bag broccoli
- Cooking spray

### Instructions
1. Spray the chicken chunks and air fry at 400°F for 10–12 min, shaking once, until 165°F inside.
2. Microwave the rice and the broccoli.
3. Toss the hot chicken with the sauce in a bowl.
4. Serve over rice with the broccoli.

### Notes
- Sprinkle on red pepper flakes if you want it hotter.
