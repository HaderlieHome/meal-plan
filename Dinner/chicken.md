## Instant Pot Japanese Chicken Curry

**Serves:** 4 (2 meals for two) · **Time:** 40 min (hands-on 15) · **Equipment:** Instant Pot, Rice Cooker · **Est. cost:** $16 total

| Per serving | Amount | % of 1,600 day | % of 2,000 day |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Calories | 601 kcal | 38% | 30% |
| Protein | 41 g | 34% | 27% |
| Carbs | 62 g | 39% | 31% |
| Fat | 22 g | 42% | 33% |
| Fiber | 8.5 g | 22% | 22% |
| Sodium | 1,195 mg | 52% | 52% |

**Storage:** 🧊 Freezer-friendly — curry keeps 4 days in the fridge or 3 months frozen (make the rice fresh).

**Why it's filling:** the rich, thick curry sauce coats a big bowl that is half cauliflower rice, and steamed broccoli adds bulk for almost no calories.

### Ingredients
- 1½ lb boneless skinless chicken thighs, cut into bite-size pieces
- 1 large onion, sliced
- 2 carrots, cut into chunks
- 1 small russet potato (~5 oz), cut into chunks
- 1 box (3.2 oz / 92 g) S&B Golden Curry roux, mild or medium (or ~90 g of a larger box). This is the amount the box sizes for 2½ cups water
- 2½ cups water
- 1 tsp oil
- ¾ cup dry white rice (short grain or jasmine)
- 1 lb frozen riced cauliflower
- 12 oz frozen broccoli florets

### Instructions
1. Start the rice cooker: ¾ cup rice plus water to the line. Put the frozen broccoli in the steaming basket on top.
2. Instant Pot on **Sauté**: heat the oil, add the chicken and onion, and stir for 3–4 min until the chicken is no longer pink on the outside.
3. Add the carrots, potato and 2½ cups of water. Scrape up any browned bits. Cancel Sauté.
4. Pressure cook on **High for 8 min**, then quick release.
5. Switch back to **Sauté**, break up the roux blocks and stir them in until melted. Simmer 3–5 min until thick.
6. Microwave the riced cauliflower according to the bag, then fold it into the cooked rice 50/50.
7. Serve the curry over the rice mix, with broccoli on the side.

### Notes
- Swaps: chicken breast works too (a little leaner). Add a spoonful of *fukujinzuke* (pickled radish relish) or a squeeze of honey for a sweeter, more authentic curry.
- Sodium comes almost entirely from the roux (about 1,000 mg of the 1,195 per serving). Use ¾ of the box with 2 cups of water to cut about 250 mg per serving.
- Freezing: portion the curry into containers and freeze. Thaw overnight in the fridge, then microwave covered with a splash of water, stirring halfway. It tastes better on day 2.

## Air Fryer Chicken Katsu with Cabbage Slaw

**Serves:** 2 · **Time:** 30 min (hands-on 15) · **Equipment:** Air Fryer, Rice Cooker · **Est. cost:** $9 total

| Per serving | Amount | % of 1,600 day | % of 2,000 day |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Calories | 607 kcal | 38% | 30% |
| Protein | 51 g | 42% | 34% |
| Carbs | 71 g | 44% | 36% |
| Fat | 12 g | 23% | 18% |
| Fiber | 8 g | 21% | 21% |
| Sodium | 1,035 mg | 45% | 45% |

**Storage:** 🔥 Cook fresh — best eaten right away. Leftovers keep 3 days and re-crisp in the air fryer.

**Why it's filling:** crispy, deep-fried-tasting chicken without the oil, with a huge pile of crunchy cabbage slaw, the traditional katsu side.

### Ingredients
- 12 oz boneless skinless chicken breast (2 small breasts), butterflied or pounded to ½ inch
- ¼ tsp salt, pepper
- 2 tbsp flour
- 1 egg, beaten (a thin coating only)
- ¾ cup panko
- Oil spray
- 2 tbsp tonkatsu sauce (Bulldog), or ketchup mixed with Worcestershire
- **Slaw:** 4 cups shredded cabbage (or a bagged mix), 1 carrot shredded, 1½ tbsp light mayo, 1 tbsp rice vinegar
- ⅓ cup dry white rice + 8 oz frozen riced cauliflower

### Instructions
1. Start the rice in the rice cooker. Microwave the cauliflower rice and mix the two together when both are done.
2. Season the chicken with salt and pepper. Dredge it in flour, then egg, then panko, pressing the panko on firmly.
3. Spray the air fryer basket and the chicken well with oil. Air fry at **400°F for 12–14 min**, flipping halfway, until the chicken reaches 165°F inside.
4. While it cooks, toss the cabbage, carrot, mayo and vinegar together.
5. Slice the katsu into strips and drizzle with tonkatsu sauce. Serve with the slaw and rice.

### Notes
- Egg is only a thin binder here, which is allowed under the egg rule. To skip it, use 2 tbsp Greek yogurt thinned with a splash of milk instead.
- Pork loin cutlets work the same way (tonkatsu).
- Leftovers: reheat in the **air fryer at 375°F for 4–5 min** so it crisps back up (never the microwave). Store the slaw separately.

## Crock Pot Thai Peanut Chicken

**Serves:** 4 (2 meals for two) · **Time:** 4–6 hr (hands-on 10) · **Equipment:** Crock Pot, Rice Cooker · **Est. cost:** $17 total

| Per serving | Amount | % of 1,600 day | % of 2,000 day |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Calories | 562 kcal | 35% | 28% |
| Protein | 51 g | 42% | 34% |
| Carbs | 52 g | 32% | 26% |
| Fat | 17 g | 32% | 25% |
| Fiber | 8 g | 21% | 21% |
| Sodium | 854 mg | 37% | 37% |

**Storage:** 🧊 Freezer-friendly — keeps 4 days in the fridge or 3 months frozen (freeze without the rice).

**Why it's filling:** a creamy, rich-tasting peanut sauce made with light coconut milk, served over lots of peppers and broccoli.

### Ingredients
- 1½ lb boneless skinless chicken breast
- ½ can (about ¾ cup) light coconut milk
- 3 tbsp creamy peanut butter
- 3 tbsp low-sodium soy sauce
- 1 tbsp honey
- 1 tbsp Thai red curry paste
- 3 cloves garlic, minced (or 1 tsp jarred)
- 1 tsp grated ginger (or jarred)
- Juice of 1 lime
- 2 red bell peppers, sliced
- 1 lb frozen broccoli florets
- ¾ cup dry jasmine rice + 12 oz frozen riced cauliflower
- 2 tbsp chopped peanuts, plus cilantro and green onion to garnish

### Instructions
1. Whisk the coconut milk, peanut butter, soy sauce, honey, curry paste, garlic and ginger together in the crock pot.
2. Add the chicken and bell peppers and turn to coat. Cook on **Low 4–6 hr** or **High 2½–3 hr**.
3. For the last 30 min, add the frozen broccoli on top and put the lid back on.
4. Shred the chicken with two forks right in the pot, then stir in the lime juice.
5. Make the rice in the rice cooker, then mix it 50/50 with the microwaved cauliflower rice. Serve topped with peanuts and herbs.

### Notes
- To cut about 60 kcal per serving, replace the peanut butter with 6 tbsp PB2 powder (it also adds 3 g protein).
- Swap in thighs for a richer sauce, at about +40 kcal per serving.
- Freezing: freeze the shredded chicken and sauce in portions, without the rice. Thaw overnight in the fridge and microwave covered, stirring halfway. Add fresh lime and peanuts after reheating.

## BBQ Shredded Chicken Loaded Baked Potatoes

**Serves:** 4 (2 meals for two) · **Time:** 50 min (hands-on 10) · **Equipment:** Instant Pot, Air Fryer · **Est. cost:** $15 total

| Per serving | Amount | % of 1,600 day | % of 2,000 day |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Calories | 560 kcal | 35% | 28% |
| Protein | 57 g | 48% | 38% |
| Carbs | 57 g | 36% | 28% |
| Fat | 11 g | 21% | 16% |
| Fiber | 5.5 g | 14% | 14% |
| Sodium | 824 mg | 36% | 36% |

**Storage:** 🥡 Fridge prep — 4 days. The BBQ chicken alone is 🧊 freezer-friendly for 3 months.

**Why it's filling:** potatoes are one of the most filling foods per calorie, and this is a fully loaded baked potato: Greek yogurt "sour cream", bacon, cheese and a mountain of BBQ chicken.

### Ingredients
- 1½ lb boneless skinless chicken breast
- ½ cup BBQ sauce (e.g. Sweet Baby Ray's), divided
- ½ cup water
- 4 medium russet potatoes (~6 oz each), scrubbed
- Oil spray and a pinch of salt for the potato skins
- 1 cup plain nonfat Greek yogurt, with a pinch of garlic powder and salt
- 4 slices center-cut bacon, cooked and crumbled (or 4 tbsp real bacon bits)
- 4 green onions, sliced
- ½ cup reduced-fat shredded cheddar
- 1 lb frozen broccoli, steamed

### Instructions
1. **Potatoes:** prick with a fork, spray with oil and salt lightly. Air fry at **400°F for 40–45 min** (in two rounds if they don't all fit), until a knife slides in easily.
2. **Chicken (at the same time):** put the chicken, ¼ cup BBQ sauce and ½ cup water in the Instant Pot. Pressure cook on **High for 10 min**, natural release 5 min.
3. Shred the chicken in the pot, drain off most of the liquid, and stir in the remaining ¼ cup BBQ sauce.
4. Split the potatoes open and fluff the insides. Top each with a quarter of the chicken, ¼ cup yogurt, cheese, bacon and green onion. Serve with broccoli.

### Notes
- Air fry the bacon: **400°F for 7–9 min**, then crumble it.
- To get more fiber, eat the skin and add a scoop of black beans, or use sweet potatoes.
- If the grill works, grill the chicken breasts with BBQ sauce and slice them instead of shredding.
- Prep: store the chicken, potatoes and toppings separately. Reheat the potato in the **air fryer at 375°F for 6–8 min** so the skin re-crisps.
- Freezing: freeze the BBQ chicken in portions (not the potatoes). Thaw overnight in the fridge and microwave.

## Instant Pot Avgolemono Chicken Soup

**Serves:** 4 (2 meals for two) · **Time:** 35 min (hands-on 15) · **Equipment:** Instant Pot · **Est. cost:** $16 total

| Per serving | Amount | % of 1,600 day | % of 2,000 day |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Calories | 490 kcal | 31% | 24% |
| Protein | 56 g | 47% | 37% |
| Carbs | 41 g | 26% | 20% |
| Fat | 11 g | 21% | 16% |
| Fiber | 6 g | 16% | 16% |
| Sodium | 1,108 mg | 48% | 48% |

**Storage:** 🥡 Fridge prep — 4 days. Don't freeze (the egg-lemon broth separates).

**Why it's filling:** a big, silky bowl of soup that is mostly broth and vegetables. Pureed white beans make it creamier and add fiber, which lets the orzo be cut back.

### Ingredients
- 1½ lb boneless skinless chicken breast
- 8 cups low-sodium chicken broth
- 1 onion, diced; 2 carrots, diced; 2 celery ribs, diced (or a 10 oz tub of pre-chopped mirepoix)
- 1 can (15 oz) cannellini beans, rinsed
- ½ cup dry orzo
- ½ tsp salt, plus pepper
- 2 eggs
- ⅓ cup lemon juice (2–3 lemons)
- 5 oz baby spinach
- 2 oz crumbled feta, for topping
- Fresh dill (optional)

### Instructions
1. Put the chicken, broth, vegetables, beans, salt and pepper in the Instant Pot. Pressure cook on **High for 10 min**, then quick release.
2. Take out the chicken and shred it. Switch to **Sauté**, add the orzo and simmer 6–7 min until tender.
3. Scoop out 1 cup of beans and broth and blend it until smooth (a stick blender or the Ninja works). Stir it back in to thicken the soup.
4. **Temper the eggs:** whisk the eggs and lemon juice in a bowl. Slowly whisk in 2 ladles of hot broth, then stir the mixture back into the pot. **Cancel Sauté first** so the soup doesn't boil and scramble the eggs.
5. Stir in the chicken and spinach until wilted. Top with feta and dill.

### Notes
- The eggs dissolve into the broth to thicken it, so they aren't "visible egg" and pass the egg rule.
- Orzo keeps absorbing broth. For leftovers, add a splash of broth when reheating.
- Reheat gently on the stove or in the microwave at 70% power, without boiling.

## Protein-Pasta Chicken Alfredo with Broccoli

**Serves:** 2 · **Time:** 25 min (hands-on 10) · **Equipment:** Instant Pot · **Est. cost:** $10 total

| Per serving | Amount | % of 1,600 day | % of 2,000 day |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Calories | 558 kcal | 35% | 28% |
| Protein | 63 g | 52% | 42% |
| Carbs | 58 g | 36% | 29% |
| Fat | 10 g | 19% | 15% |
| Fiber | 9.5 g | 25% | 25% |
| Sodium | 532 mg | 23% | 23% |

**Storage:** 🔥 Cook fresh — the yogurt sauce gets grainy when reheated. Leftovers keep 3 days if needed.

**Why it's filling:** a creamy, cheesy pasta bowl where the broccoli roughly doubles the volume. A Greek yogurt and parmesan sauce replaces the heavy cream and butter.

### Ingredients
- 10 oz boneless skinless chicken breast, cut into bite-size pieces
- 4 oz Barilla Protein+ penne (or chickpea pasta)
- 2 cups low-sodium chicken broth
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 12 oz broccoli florets (fresh or frozen)
- **Sauce:** ¾ cup plain nonfat Greek yogurt, ½ cup 1% milk, ⅓ cup grated parmesan, 1 tsp cornstarch, black pepper, pinch of nutmeg (optional)

### Instructions
1. Put the chicken, pasta, garlic and broth in the Instant Pot, pushing the pasta under the liquid. Pressure cook on **High for 4 min**, then quick release. Check that the chicken reaches 165°F, and if not, simmer on Sauté 1–2 min.
2. Add the broccoli on top, put the lid back on (no pressure) and let it steam for 4–5 min.
3. Whisk the sauce ingredients together until smooth. With the pot on **Keep Warm** (not Sauté, because boiling curdles yogurt), stir the sauce in until creamy.
4. Season with pepper and serve.

### Notes
- If there's extra liquid after pressure cooking, keep it. It thins the sauce.
- Add sun-dried tomatoes or spinach for variety.
- Kept at 2 servings on purpose. If you do have leftovers, reheat with a splash of milk at 70% microwave power and stir often.

## Instant Pot Chicken Marsala

**Serves:** 4 (Sunday batch → Monday and Tuesday for two) · **Time:** 40 min (hands-on 20) · **Equipment:** Instant Pot · **Est. cost:** $20 total

| Per serving | Amount | % of 1,600 day | % of 2,000 day |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Calories | 605 kcal | 38% | 30% |
| Protein | 64 g | 53% | 43% |
| Carbs | 54 g | 34% | 27% |
| Fat | 13 g | 25% | 19% |
| Fiber | 9 g | 24% | 24% |
| Sodium | 739 mg | 32% | 32% |

**Storage:** 🧊 Freezer-friendly — chicken and sauce keep 4 days in the fridge or 3 months frozen (cook the pasta fresh or store it separately).

**Why it's filling:** a restaurant-style plate with a full pound of mushrooms in the sauce, protein pasta, and a big side of green beans.

### Ingredients
- 2 lb boneless skinless chicken breast, sliced into thin cutlets
- 1 tsp salt, pepper, 1 tsp dried thyme
- 1 tbsp olive oil + 1 tbsp butter
- 16 oz cremini mushrooms, sliced (buy pre-sliced)
- 1 shallot or ½ onion, diced
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- ¾ cup dry Marsala wine
- ½ cup low-sodium chicken broth
- 2 tbsp cornstarch mixed with 2 tbsp cold water
- 6 oz Barilla Protein+ spaghetti
- 1 lb green beans (fresh, or frozen steam-in-bag)
- Parsley (optional)

### Instructions
1. Season the chicken with salt, pepper and thyme. **Sauté** in oil for 2 min per side in batches (don't cook through), then set aside.
2. Add the butter, mushrooms and shallot and sauté 5 min. Add the garlic, then pour in the Marsala and scrape up the browned bits. Simmer 2 min.
3. Add the broth and return the chicken. Pressure cook on **High for 6 min**, natural release 5 min.
4. Take out the chicken. On **Sauté**, stir in the cornstarch slurry and simmer 1–2 min until glossy and thick. Return the chicken.
5. Meanwhile, boil the pasta on the stove and steam the green beans (microwave bag). Serve the chicken and sauce over the pasta.

### Notes
- Most of the wine's alcohol cooks off. The numbers above count about 150 kcal of wine for the whole batch.
- Instead of pasta, serve over mashed potatoes or half cauliflower mash, for about the same calories.
- Freezing: freeze the chicken and sauce in portions, without the pasta. Thaw overnight in the fridge and microwave covered. For fridge leftovers, pasta reheats best tossed in the sauce.

## Instant Pot Butternut Squash Risotto with Chicken

**Serves:** 4 (2 meals for two) · **Time:** 40 min (hands-on 15) · **Equipment:** Instant Pot · **Est. cost:** $17 total

| Per serving | Amount | % of 1,600 day | % of 2,000 day |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Calories | 573 kcal | 36% | 29% |
| Protein | 50 g | 42% | 33% |
| Carbs | 67 g | 42% | 34% |
| Fat | 11 g | 21% | 16% |
| Fiber | 6.5 g | 17% | 17% |
| Sodium | 757 mg | 33% | 33% |

**Storage:** 🥡 Fridge prep — 4 days. Don't freeze (the rice turns grainy).

**Why it's filling:** squash melts into the rice so the risotto is extra creamy, and it takes less arborio than usual. The Instant Pot means no stirring.

### Ingredients
- 1½ lb boneless skinless chicken breast
- 1 cup arborio rice (scant)
- 1½ lb peeled, cubed butternut squash (buy pre-cut or frozen)
- 1 onion, diced
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 3½ cups low-sodium chicken broth (the squash releases liquid too)
- ½ tsp salt, pepper, ½ tsp dried sage (or a few fresh leaves)
- 1 cup frozen peas
- 4 oz baby spinach
- ½ cup grated parmesan
- 1 tbsp butter

### Instructions
1. On **Sauté**, melt the butter and cook the onion 3 min. Add the garlic and rice and stir 1 min.
2. Add the broth, squash, salt, pepper and sage. Cut the chicken into 2-inch chunks and lay them on top. Pressure cook on **High for 6 min**, natural release 5 min, then release the rest.
3. Take out the chicken (it should be 165°F) and shred it. Stir the risotto hard for 1 min to mash some of the squash into the rice.
4. Stir in the peas, spinach, parmesan and shredded chicken. Let it sit 2 min, and loosen with a splash of broth if it's too thick.

### Notes
- To add fiber, stir in a can of rinsed white beans with the peas.
- Risotto firms up when chilled, so reheat with a splash of broth.
