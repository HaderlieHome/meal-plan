## Instant Pot Turkey-Lentil Sloppy Joes

**Serves:** 4 (2 meals for two) · **Time:** 35 min (hands-on 10) · **Equipment:** Instant Pot · **Est. cost:** $13 total

| Per serving | Amount | % of 1,600 day | % of 2,000 day |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Calories | 519 kcal | 32% | 26% |
| Protein | 40 g | 33% | 27% |
| Carbs | 68 g | 42% | 34% |
| Fat | 12 g | 23% | 18% |
| Fiber | 12 g | 32% | 32% |
| Sodium | 816 mg | 35% | 35% |

**Storage:** 🧊 Freezer-friendly — keeps 4 days in the fridge or 3 months frozen (buns and slaw fresh).

**Why it's filling:** lentils stretch the meat, so each sandwich is piled high and packed with fiber. The crunchy slaw on the side adds volume.

### Ingredients
- 1 lb 93% lean ground turkey
- ¾ cup dry brown or green lentils, rinsed
- 1 onion, diced
- 1 green bell pepper, diced
- 1 can (15 oz) no-salt-added tomato sauce
- 2 tbsp tomato paste
- 2 tbsp brown sugar
- 1 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
- 1 tbsp yellow mustard
- ½ tsp salt, 1 tsp garlic powder, 1 tsp chili powder
- 1½ cups water
- 4 whole wheat hamburger buns
- 4 cups shredded cabbage or coleslaw mix, tossed with a splash of vinegar and a pinch of salt

### Instructions
1. On **Sauté**, brown the turkey with the onion and pepper for 5 min, breaking it up.
2. Add the lentils, water, Worcestershire, mustard, brown sugar and spices. Stir and scrape the bottom.
3. Pour the tomato sauce and tomato paste **on top without stirring**, which prevents a burn warning.
4. Pressure cook on **High for 12 min**, natural release 10 min. Stir. If it's runny, simmer on Sauté 3–5 min.
5. Pile onto buns and serve the slaw on the side, or on the sandwich.

### Notes
- Starch swap: serve over a baked potato instead of a bun, at about the same calories and a bit more filling.
- Kid-classic flavor: add 2 tbsp ketchup (+10 kcal per serving).
- Freezing: freeze the filling flat in quart bags, one per meal for two. Thaw overnight in the fridge and microwave covered, stirring halfway. Toast the buns in the air fryer at 350°F for 2 min.

## Taco Salad Bowls (Dad's Doritos Remix)

**Serves:** 4 (2 meals for two) · **Time:** 25 min (hands-on 15) · **Equipment:** Stovetop skillet or Instant Pot (Sauté) · **Est. cost:** $22 total

| Per serving | Amount | % of 1,600 day | % of 2,000 day |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Calories | 587 kcal | 37% | 29% |
| Protein | 48 g | 40% | 32% |
| Carbs | 49 g | 31% | 24% |
| Fat | 24 g | 45% | 36% |
| Fiber | 12 g | 32% | 32% |
| Sodium | 1,025 mg | 45% | 45% |

**Storage:** 🥡 Fridge prep — 4 days, with the meat, greens and salsa stored separately. The taco meat alone is 🧊 freezer-friendly for 3 months.

**Why it's filling:** a huge bowl, almost 6 cups of greens and toppings per person, with a small handful of Doritos crushed on top so every bite gets the crunch.

### Ingredients
- 1½ lb 93% lean ground turkey
- 1 can (15 oz) black beans, rinsed
- **Taco seasoning:** 2 tbsp chili powder, 2 tsp cumin, 1 tsp garlic powder, 1 tsp onion powder, ¼ tsp salt, ½ cup water
- 8 cups chopped romaine
- 4 cups shredded cabbage
- 1 cup frozen corn, thawed (or charred in the skillet)
- **Pineapple salsa:** 1 cup diced pineapple mixed with ⅔ cup jarred salsa
- ½ cup plain nonfat Greek yogurt + a squeeze of lime ("sour cream")
- ½ cup reduced-fat shredded cheddar
- 2 oz Nacho Cheese Doritos (about 24 chips, or four 1 oz snack bags), crushed

### Instructions
1. Brown the turkey in a skillet for 7–8 min, until no pink remains (165°F). Add the seasoning, water and beans and simmer 2 min until saucy.
2. Stir the pineapple into the salsa.
3. Build 2 bowls for tonight: romaine and cabbage, then a quarter of the turkey and beans each, corn, pineapple salsa, cheese and yogurt. Crush the Doritos over the top just before eating.
4. Store the other half of the meat, greens, corn and salsa in separate containers for the second night. Keep the Doritos in the bag.

### Notes
- Night two takes 2 min: reheat the meat only, then assemble the bowls.
- Store the greens with a paper towel in the container so they stay crisp.
- Most of the fat comes from the turkey and Doritos. To save about 60 kcal per serving, use 99% lean turkey breast.
- Freezing: the seasoned meat and beans freeze well for 3 months. Thaw overnight in the fridge and microwave.

## Crock Pot Turkey and Bean Chili

**Serves:** 6 (3 meals for two) · **Time:** 6–8 hr (hands-on 15) · **Equipment:** Crock Pot · **Est. cost:** $20 total

| Per serving | Amount | % of 1,600 day | % of 2,000 day |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Calories | 489 kcal | 31% | 24% |
| Protein | 47 g | 39% | 31% |
| Carbs | 45 g | 28% | 22% |
| Fat | 15 g | 28% | 22% |
| Fiber | 14 g | 37% | 37% |
| Sodium | 1,234 mg | 54% | 54% |

**Storage:** 🧊 Freezer-friendly — keeps 4 days in the fridge or 4 months frozen.

**Why it's filling:** a thick, chunky bowl of meat, three cans of beans for fiber, and a zucchini that disappears into the sauce. The lowest-calorie dinner here and one of the most filling.

### Ingredients
- 2 lb 93% lean ground turkey
- 2 cans (15 oz) kidney beans, rinsed
- 1 can (15 oz) black beans, rinsed
- 1 can (28 oz) crushed tomatoes
- 1 can (14.5 oz) diced tomatoes (or Rotel for heat)
- 1 onion, diced
- 2 bell peppers, diced
- 1 zucchini, diced small
- 2 tbsp chili powder, 1 tbsp cumin, 1 tsp smoked paprika, 1 tsp garlic powder, 1 tsp dried oregano, 1 tsp salt
- **Toppings:** 1½ cups plain nonfat Greek yogurt (¼ cup per bowl), sliced green onions

### Instructions
1. Brown the turkey with the onion in a skillet or the Instant Pot on Sauté for 6–7 min. Don't skip this: USDA advises browning ground meat before slow cooking so it heats through safely.
2. Put everything except the toppings in the crock pot and stir.
3. Cook on **Low 6–8 hr** or **High 3–4 hr**.
4. Serve topped with Greek yogurt and green onions.

### Notes
- **Capacity:** this batch is about 4 quarts, so it needs a 6 qt crock pot. With a 4 qt pot, halve the recipe.
- **Instant Pot version:** brown the turkey on Sauté, add everything else with the tomatoes on top, then High pressure 10 min with 10 min natural release. That's right at the 6 qt pot's ⅔ max fill line, so leave out the zucchini if it's over.
- **Sodium** (about 1,230 mg per serving) comes mostly from the salt and canned goods. Use no-salt-added tomatoes and beans, or cut the salt to ½ tsp, to get it under 900 mg.
- Starch lovers: add ½ cup cooked rice or a small baked potato underneath (+100–150 kcal).
- Freezing: freeze in single-serve containers. Thaw overnight in the fridge, or microwave from frozen at 50% power for 5 min, then full power until hot, stirring halfway. It's a great grab-and-go lunch too.

## Korean Gochujang Turkey Rice Bowls

**Serves:** 4 (2 meals for two) · **Time:** 25 min (hands-on 15) · **Equipment:** Rice Cooker, stovetop skillet · **Est. cost:** $19 total

| Per serving | Amount | % of 1,600 day | % of 2,000 day |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Calories | 591 kcal | 37% | 30% |
| Protein | 45 g | 38% | 30% |
| Carbs | 59 g | 37% | 30% |
| Fat | 21 g | 40% | 31% |
| Fiber | 8 g | 21% | 21% |
| Sodium | 1,011 mg | 44% | 44% |

**Storage:** 🥡 Fridge prep — 4 days, with the vegetables stored separately. The sauced turkey alone is 🧊 freezer-friendly for 3 months.

**Why it's filling:** sweet-spicy, sticky turkey over a rice bowl that's half cauliflower, topped with lots of cool, crunchy vegetables and kimchi for gut health.

### Ingredients
- 1½ lb 93% lean ground turkey
- 1½ cups frozen shelled edamame
- **Sauce:** 3 tbsp gochujang, 2 tbsp low-sodium soy sauce, 1 tbsp honey, 2 tsp sesame oil, 3 cloves garlic (minced), 1 tsp grated ginger
- ¾ cup dry short-grain or jasmine rice + 12 oz frozen riced cauliflower
- 1 English cucumber, thinly sliced
- 2 cups shredded carrots
- 1 cup kimchi
- 2 tsp sesame seeds, sliced green onions

### Instructions
1. Start the rice in the rice cooker. Put the frozen edamame in the steaming basket on top.
2. Brown the turkey in a large skillet for 6–7 min, breaking it into crumbles.
3. Stir the sauce together, pour it over the turkey, and cook 2–3 min until sticky.
4. Microwave the cauliflower rice and mix it 50/50 with the cooked rice.
5. Build the bowls: rice mix, turkey, edamame, cucumber, carrots and kimchi. Sprinkle with sesame seeds and green onion.

### Notes
- If you'd like a runny-yolk bibimbap egg on top, that's the egg as a main feature, so skip it per the egg rule.
- Pickle the cucumbers quickly with 2 tbsp rice vinegar and a pinch of sugar for extra tang.
- Prep: store the vegetables separately and eat them cold. Reheat only the turkey and rice. Cool the rice quickly (spread it out) before refrigerating.
- Freezing: freeze the sauced turkey in portions. Thaw overnight in the fridge and microwave covered.
