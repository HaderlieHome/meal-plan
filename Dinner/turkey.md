## Crock Pot Turkey Meatball Subs

**Serves:** 4 (2 meals for two) · **Time:** 3–4 hr on Low (hands-on 5) · **Equipment:** Crock Pot, microwave or oven · **Est. cost:** ~$19 total

| Calories | 597 kcal | 37% | 30% |

| Fat | 22 g | 42% | 33% |
| Fiber | 6 g | 16% | 16% |
| Sodium | 1,715 mg | 75% | 75% |

**Storage:** 🧊 Freezer-friendly — the meatballs and sauce keep 4 days in the fridge or 3 months frozen. Build subs fresh.

**Why it's filling:** A big, messy meatball sub with melted cheese.

### Ingredients
- 20 oz bag frozen turkey meatballs
- ½ jar (24 oz) marinara sauce
- 4 hoagie rolls
- 4 slices provolone

### Instructions
1. Dump the frozen meatballs and marinara into the crock pot.
2. Cook on Low 3–4 hours (or High 2 hours).
3. Split the rolls, fill with meatballs and sauce, and top with a slice of provolone.
4. Microwave 20 sec (or broil 1–2 min) to melt the cheese.

### Notes
- Any frozen turkey or chicken meatballs work.

## Turkey Sloppy Joes

**Serves:** 4 (2 meals for two) · **Time:** 20 min (hands-on 15) · **Equipment:** Skillet · **Est. cost:** ~$12 total

| Per serving | Amount | % of 1,600 day | % of 2,000 day |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Calories | 475 kcal | 30% | 24% |
| Protein | 38 g | 32% | 25% |
| Carbs | 42 g | 26% | 21% |
| Fat | 16 g | 30% | 24% |
| Fiber | 3 g | 8% | 8% |
| Sodium | 1,092 mg | 47% | 47% |

**Storage:** 🧊 Freezer-friendly — the meat keeps 4 days in the fridge or 3 months frozen.

**Why it's filling:** A loaded sloppy joe, with the sauce straight from the can.

### Ingredients
- 1½ lb 93% lean ground turkey
- 1 can (15 oz) Manwich Original sauce
- 4 hamburger buns

### Instructions
1. Brown the turkey in a skillet over medium-high heat, breaking it up, until no pink remains (about 8 min).
2. Pour in the Manwich and simmer 5 min.
3. Spoon onto buns.

### Notes
- Add a slice of cheese if you want (about +70 kcal).

## Turkey Taco Bowls

**Serves:** 4 (2 meals for two) · **Time:** 20 min (hands-on 15) · **Equipment:** Skillet, microwave · **Est. cost:** ~$14 total

| Per serving | Amount | % of 1,600 day | % of 2,000 day |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Calories | 532 kcal | 33% | 27% |
| Protein | 42 g | 35% | 28% |
| Carbs | 45 g | 28% | 22% |
| Fat | 20 g | 38% | 30% |
| Fiber | 8 g | 21% | 21% |
| Sodium | 1,248 mg | 54% | 54% |

**Storage:** 🥡 Fridge prep — 4 days. The taco meat also freezes for 3 months.

**Why it's filling:** Taco meat, rice, beans, salsa and cheese in one big bowl.

### Ingredients
- 1½ lb 93% lean ground turkey
- 1 packet taco seasoning
- 1 pouch (8.5 oz) microwave rice
- 1 can black beans, drained
- 1 cup salsa
- ½ cup shredded Mexican cheese

### Instructions
1. Brown the turkey in a skillet until no pink remains (about 8 min).
2. Stir in the taco seasoning and water per the packet. Simmer 3 min.
3. Microwave the rice pouch and the beans (1 min in a bowl).
4. Build bowls: rice, beans, taco meat, salsa and cheese.

### Notes
- Swap the turkey for ground beef if you like; it adds about 60 kcal per bowl.
