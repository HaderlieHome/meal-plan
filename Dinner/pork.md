## Crock Pot Pulled Pork Sandwiches

**Serves:** 4 (2 meals for two) · **Time:** 6–7 hr on Low (hands-on 5) · **Equipment:** Crock Pot · **Est. cost:** ~$12 total

| Per serving | Amount | % of 1,600 day | % of 2,000 day |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Calories | 529 kcal | 33% | 26% |
| Protein | 42 g | 35% | 28% |
| Carbs | 63 g | 39% | 31% |
| Fat | 11 g | 21% | 16% |
| Fiber | 2 g | 5% | 5% |
| Sodium | 864 mg | 38% | 38% |

**Storage:** 🧊 Freezer-friendly — pork keeps 4 days in the fridge or 3 months frozen.

**Why it's filling:** A big BBQ pork sandwich for about the calories of a fast-food burger, with twice the protein.

### Ingredients
- 1½ lb boneless pork loin roast
- 1 bottle BBQ sauce (you'll use about 1 cup)
- ½ cup water
- 4 hamburger buns
- Pickle chips (optional)

### Instructions
1. Put the pork in the crock pot. Pour ½ cup BBQ sauce and the water over it.
2. Cook on Low 6–7 hr.
3. Shred the pork with two forks right in the pot. Stir in another ½ cup BBQ sauce.
4. Pile onto buns.

### Notes
- A scoop of store-bought coleslaw on top adds about 150 kcal.

## Air Fryer Pork Chops

**Serves:** 2 · **Time:** 20 min (hands-on 5) · **Equipment:** Air Fryer, microwave · **Est. cost:** ~$12 total

| Per serving | Amount | % of 1,600 day | % of 2,000 day |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Calories | 549 kcal | 34% | 27% |
| Protein | 46 g | 38% | 31% |
| Carbs | 65 g | 41% | 33% |
| Fat | 13 g | 24% | 19% |
| Fiber | 7 g | 18% | 18% |
| Sodium | 423 mg | 18% | 18% |

**Storage:** 🔥 Cook fresh — leftover chops keep 3 days in the fridge.

**Why it's filling:** A thick pork chop with rice, broccoli and applesauce, the classic plate.

### Ingredients
- 2 boneless pork chops (6 oz each, about 1 inch thick)
- Montreal steak seasoning (or any grill seasoning)
- 1 pouch (8.8 oz) microwave white rice
- 1 bag (12 oz) steam-in-bag broccoli
- 2 applesauce cups

### Instructions
1. Season both sides of the chops.
2. Air fry at 400°F for 10–12 min, flipping halfway, until 145°F inside. Rest 3 min.
3. Microwave the rice and broccoli per the packages.
4. Plate with an applesauce cup each.

## Crock Pot Pork Carnitas Tacos

**Serves:** 4 (2 meals for two) · **Time:** 6–7 hr on Low (hands-on 5) · **Equipment:** Crock Pot · **Est. cost:** ~$14 total

| Per serving | Amount | % of 1,600 day | % of 2,000 day |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Calories | 581 kcal | 36% | 29% |
| Protein | 48 g | 40% | 32% |
| Carbs | 49 g | 30% | 24% |
| Fat | 21 g | 40% | 31% |
| Fiber | 4 g | 11% | 11% |
| Sodium | 1,291 mg | 56% | 56% |

**Storage:** 🧊 Freezer-friendly — pork keeps 4 days in the fridge or 3 months frozen.

**Why it's filling:** Tender shredded pork in two tacos each, with cheese and tangy salsa verde.

### Ingredients
- 1½ lb boneless pork loin roast
- 1 cup jarred salsa verde
- 8 flour tortillas (8-inch soft taco size)
- ½ cup shredded Mexican cheese
- 1 lime (optional)

### Instructions
1. Put the pork in the crock pot and pour the salsa verde over it.
2. Cook on Low 6–7 hr.
3. Shred the pork with two forks in the pot and stir it into the juices.
4. Warm the tortillas 20 sec in the microwave. Fill with pork, cheese and a squeeze of lime.

## Pork Yakisoba

**Serves:** 2 · **Time:** 15 min (hands-on 15) · **Equipment:** Skillet · **Est. cost:** ~$9 total

| Per serving | Amount | % of 1,600 day | % of 2,000 day |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Calories | 576 kcal | 36% | 29% |
| Protein | 46 g | 38% | 31% |
| Carbs | 57 g | 36% | 29% |
| Fat | 18 g | 34% | 27% |
| Fiber | 6 g | 17% | 17% |
| Sodium | 1,217 mg | 53% | 53% |

**Storage:** 🥡 Fridge prep — 3 days. Reheat in the microwave with a splash of water.

**Why it's filling:** A big plate of Japanese stir-fried noodles with pork and cabbage.

### Ingredients
- ¾ lb boneless pork loin (or pork chops), sliced into thin strips
- 2 blocks Maruchan Yakisoba noodles with their sauce packets (refrigerated section)
- 4 cups bagged coleslaw mix
- 2 tsp oil

### Instructions
1. Heat the oil in a big skillet on medium-high. Cook the pork strips 4–5 min until no pink remains (145°F).
2. Add the coleslaw mix and cook 2 min.
3. Add the noodles and the water the package calls for. Stir until the noodles loosen, 2–3 min.
4. Stir in the sauce packets and serve.

### Notes
- Nutrition assumes the refrigerated Maruchan Yakisoba packs; check the label if yours differ.
