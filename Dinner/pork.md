## Crock Pot Pork Carnitas Bowls

**Serves:** 6 (3 dinners for two) · **Time:** 8 hr on Low (hands-on 20) · **Equipment:** Crock Pot, Rice Cooker, Air Fryer · **Est. cost:** ~$24 total (~$4/serving)

| Per serving | Amount | % of 1,600 day | % of 2,000 day |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Calories | 510 kcal | 32% | 26% |
| Protein | 47 g | 40% | 32% |
| Carbs | 58 g | 36% | 29% |
| Fat | 10 g | 18% | 14% |
| Fiber | 11 g | 30% | 30% |
| Sodium | 1,320 mg | 57% | 57% |

**Storage:** 🧊 Freezer-friendly (pork only)

**Why it's filling:** beans, rice and a big pile of crunchy cabbage turn 5 oz of lean pork into a burrito bowl, with no tortilla needed.

### Ingredients
- 2 lb boneless pork loin roast (or pork sirloin roast), trimmed of visible fat, cut into 4 chunks
- ½ cup orange juice + ¼ cup lime juice (about 2 limes)
- 1 onion, quartered, and 5 cloves garlic, smashed
- **Spice mix:** 2 tsp cumin, 1 tsp chili powder, 1 tsp dried oregano, 1½ tsp salt
- 1 cup dry brown rice (makes about 3 cups cooked, ½ cup per serving)
- 2 cans (15 oz) black beans, drained and rinsed
- 1½ cups salsa (¼ cup per serving)
- 6 cups shredded cabbage (1 cup per serving)
- **Lime crema:** scant 1 cup nonfat Greek yogurt + squeeze of lime. Cilantro to finish

### Instructions
1. Rub the pork with the spice mix. Put the onion and garlic in the crock pot, set the pork on top and pour over the orange and lime juice.
2. **Cook on Low for 8 hr** (or High for 4–5 hr) until it shreds easily. Shred it with 2 forks right in the juices.
3. **Crisp it:** spread 2 servings of pork in the air fryer and **air fry at 400°F for 5–6 min**, until the edges brown. This is what makes it taste like carnitas.
4. Cook the brown rice in the rice cooker (use the package's brown-rice water ratio). Warm the beans.
5. Bowl: rice, beans, cabbage, pork, salsa and crema.

### Notes
- Pork shoulder is the classic cut, but it's about twice the fat. Loin keeps it lean, and the juices stop it from drying out.
- Swap: use it for tacos (2 corn tortillas instead of the rice) or on a big salad.
- Storage: store the pork in its juices. It keeps 3–4 days in the fridge or 3 months frozen. Re-crisp it in the air fryer from the fridge.
- Freezing: freeze the shredded pork with a few spoonfuls of its juices in 2-serving zip bags, pressed flat. Thaw overnight in the fridge, then air fry at 400°F for 6–7 min to crisp. Freeze the rice separately (it reheats fine); keep the beans, cabbage, salsa and crema fresh.

## Air Fryer Teriyaki Pork Tenderloin

**Serves:** 2 · **Time:** 35 min (hands-on 10) · **Equipment:** Air Fryer, Rice Cooker (with steaming basket) · **Est. cost:** ~$10 total

| Per serving | Amount | % of 1,600 day | % of 2,000 day |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Calories | 580 kcal | 36% | 29% |
| Protein | 59 g | 49% | 39% |
| Carbs | 63 g | 40% | 32% |
| Fat | 11 g | 20% | 16% |
| Fiber | 9 g | 24% | 24% |
| Sodium | 1,100 mg | 48% | 48% |

**Storage:** 🥡 Fridge prep — 3–4 days

**Why it's filling:** the rice cooker makes the rice and steams a full pound of broccoli and carrots at the same time, for a full plate with 2 things to wash.

### Ingredients
- 1 pork tenderloin (about 1 lb). Costco sells 2-packs, so freeze the second one
- **Teriyaki glaze:** 3 Tbsp low-sodium soy sauce, 1½ Tbsp honey, 1 tsp grated ginger, 2 cloves garlic (minced), 1 tsp cornstarch
- ⅓ cup dry brown rice
- 1 lb broccoli florets + 2 carrots, sliced into coins
- 1 tsp sesame seeds, sliced green onion (optional)

### Instructions
1. Start the brown rice in the rice cooker. About 12 minutes before it's done, put the broccoli and carrots in the steaming basket on top.
2. Whisk the glaze in a microwave-safe bowl and **microwave for 45–60 sec** until it thickens. Stir halfway.
3. Pat the tenderloin dry and brush with a third of the glaze. **Air fry at 400°F for 18–22 min**, flipping and brushing with more glaze halfway, to 145°F.
4. Rest 5 minutes, slice into medallions and drizzle with the rest of the glaze. Serve with the rice and veg and top with sesame seeds.

### Notes
- Protein runs a bit high (about 59 g/serving) because tenderloins come in 1 lb packs. Save a few slices for a lunch salad if you want to trim it.
- Kept at 2 servings because the rice cooker's steaming basket only holds about 1 lb of veg. Freeze the second tenderloin from the Costco 2-pack raw for another night.
- Swap: chicken breast works the same way (air fry 18–20 min to 165°F).
- Storage: 3–4 days in the fridge. Reheat the slices covered in the microwave so they don't dry out.

## Egg Roll in a Bowl

**Serves:** 4 (2 dinners for two) · **Time:** 25 min (hands-on 15) · **Equipment:** Instant Pot on Sauté or a Dutch oven (2 lb of slaw is too much for most skillets) · **Est. cost:** ~$20 total

| Per serving | Amount | % of 1,600 day | % of 2,000 day |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Calories | 550 kcal | 34% | 28% |
| Protein | 55 g | 46% | 37% |
| Carbs | 27 g | 17% | 14% |
| Fat | 24 g | 45% | 36% |
| Fiber | 10 g | 26% | 26% |
| Sodium | 970 mg | 42% | 42% |

**Storage:** 🥡 Fridge prep — 3 days

**Why it's filling:** a whole pound of coleslaw mix cooks down into a big, savory bowl with all the egg roll flavor and none of the wrapper or frying. There's no egg in it; the name comes from the filling.

### Ingredients
- 1 lb ground pork + 1 lb 99% lean ground turkey. The pork brings the flavor and the turkey keeps it lean
- 2 bags (16 oz each) coleslaw mix (cabbage and carrots)
- 8 green onions, sliced (whites and greens separated)
- 6 cloves garlic, minced, and 2 Tbsp grated ginger
- ¼ cup low-sodium soy sauce
- 2 frozen bags (about 10 oz each) cauliflower rice, to serve
- **Sriracha "mayo":** ½ cup nonfat Greek yogurt + 4 tsp sriracha

### Instructions
1. Brown the pork and turkey in the Instant Pot on **Sauté** (or a Dutch oven over medium-high heat), about 8 minutes, until no pink remains (160°F). The pork fat is enough oil.
2. Add the scallion whites, garlic and ginger and cook 1 minute.
3. Add the coleslaw mix one bag at a time with the soy sauce. Toss 5–6 minutes until wilted but still a little crunchy.
4. Microwave the cauliflower rice. Serve the pork and cabbage over it, drizzle with sriracha mayo and top with the scallion greens.

### Notes
- Swap: all ground pork is more authentic but adds about 160 kcal and 21 g fat per serving. All turkey is leaner still.
- Add a drizzle of toasted sesame oil (40 kcal/tsp) if you have the budget.
- Meal prep: pack the pork and cabbage separately from the cauliflower rice, and keep the sriracha mayo in its own small container.
- Storage: 3 days in the fridge. The cabbage softens but reheats fine in the microwave.
