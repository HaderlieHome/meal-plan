# Plant-Based Dinners

Meatless curries and stir-fries, with protein boosted by lentils, tofu, edamame and Greek yogurt. They're cheap, very high in fiber and gut-friendly.

- [Instant Pot Chana Masala with Raita](#instant-pot-chana-masala-with-raita): 510 kcal · 33 g protein

- [Instant Pot Red Lentil Coconut Curry](#instant-pot-red-lentil-coconut-curry): 530 kcal · 37 g protein

## Instant Pot Chana Masala with Raita

**Serves:** 4 (2 dinners for two) · **Time:** 35 min (hands-on 15) · **Equipment:** Instant Pot, Rice Cooker · **Est. cost:** ~$11 total (~$2.75/serving)

| Per serving | Amount | % of 1,600 day | % of 2,000 day |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Calories | 510 kcal | 32% | 26% |
| Protein | 33 g | 28% | 22% |
| Carbs | 78 g | 49% | 39% |
| Fat | 8 g | 15% | 12% |
| Fiber | 18 g | 47% | 47% |
| Sodium | 1,000 mg | 43% | 43% |

**Storage:** 🧊 Freezer-friendly (curry only; make raita fresh)

**Why it's filling:** chickpeas plus red lentils melted into the sauce give about 18 g of fiber per bowl, and a generous scoop of yogurt raita adds about 11 g of protein.

### Ingredients
- 2 cans (15 oz) chickpeas, drained and rinsed
- ¾ cup dry red lentils, rinsed (they dissolve and thicken the sauce)
- 1 can (14.5 oz) diced tomatoes + 2 Tbsp tomato paste
- 1 onion (diced), 4 cloves garlic, 2 Tbsp grated ginger
- 2 Tbsp spices: 1 Tbsp garam masala, 1 tsp cumin, 1 tsp turmeric, 1 tsp coriander, ½ tsp cayenne (to taste)
- 1 Tbsp olive oil, ¾ tsp salt, 2 cups water
- 5 oz baby spinach
- ⅓ cup dry basmati rice (a small scoop per serving)
- **Raita:** 2 cups nonfat Greek yogurt + ½ grated cucumber + pinch of salt, cumin and mint or cilantro

### Instructions
1. Instant Pot on **Sauté** with the oil: cook the onion 4 minutes. Add the garlic, ginger, spices and tomato paste and stir for 1 minute.
2. Add the water and scrape the bottom well. Add the lentils and chickpeas, then pour the diced tomatoes on top without stirring.
3. Seal and **Pressure Cook High 10 min**, then natural release for 10 minutes. Stir in the spinach until wilted and add salt to taste.
4. Cook the basmati in the rice cooker. Stir the raita together.
5. Serve the curry over a small scoop of rice with ½ cup raita on top.

### Notes
- Protein lands at about 33 g, just under target. Add another ¼ cup of raita per bowl for +5 g.
- Swap: cauliflower rice instead of basmati to save about 50 kcal.
- Storage: 4 days in the fridge or 3 months frozen. It tastes better on day 2. Keep the raita separate (it keeps 3 days and doesn't freeze).
- Freezing: freeze the curry in 2-serving containers, leaving 1" of headspace. Thaw overnight in the fridge, or microwave from frozen covered, stirring every 2 minutes. Add a splash of water, since chickpeas thicken the sauce.

## Air Fryer Crispy Tofu General Tso's

**Serves:** 2 · **Time:** 35 min (hands-on 15) · **Equipment:** Air Fryer, Rice Cooker · **Est. cost:** ~$9 total

| Per serving | Amount | % of 1,600 day | % of 2,000 day |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Calories | 550 kcal | 34% | 28% |
| Protein | 37 g | 31% | 25% |
| Carbs | 66 g | 41% | 33% |
| Fat | 17 g | 32% | 25% |
| Fiber | 13 g | 34% | 34% |
| Sodium | 920 mg | 40% | 40% |

**Storage:** 🥡 Fridge prep — 3 days

**Why it's filling:** crispy cornstarch-dusted tofu without deep frying, plus edamame and a full bag of broccoli over 50/50 rice and cauliflower rice.

### Ingredients
- 1 block (14 oz) extra-firm tofu, pressed 15 minutes and cubed
- 2 Tbsp cornstarch + 1 tsp oil (or spray)
- **Sauce:** 2 Tbsp low-sodium soy sauce, 1 Tbsp hoisin, 2 tsp honey, 1 Tbsp rice vinegar, 1 tsp sriracha, 2 cloves garlic, 1 tsp grated ginger, 1 tsp cornstarch + 3 Tbsp water
- 12 oz broccoli florets
- ¾ cup shelled edamame
- ¼ cup dry white rice + 2 cups frozen cauliflower rice
- 1 green onion, sliced. Optional: dried chilies

### Instructions
1. Start the rice in the rice cooker, with the broccoli and edamame in the steamer basket for the last 10 minutes.
2. Toss the tofu with the oil, then the cornstarch. **Air fry at 400°F for 15–18 min**, shaking every 5 minutes, until crisp.
3. Whisk the sauce in a microwave-safe bowl and **microwave for 60–90 sec**, stirring once, until glossy.
4. Toss the hot tofu in the sauce. Stir the microwaved cauliflower rice into the rice. Serve with the broccoli and edamame and top with green onion.

### Notes
- Freezing the tofu block overnight, then thawing it, gives it a chewier, meatier texture.
- Swap: chicken breast chunks work the same way (air fry 12–14 min to 165°F).
- Kept at 2 servings: crispy dishes lose their crunch in the fridge, and a doubled batch of tofu won't fit the 5 qt basket in a single layer.
- Storage: 3 days in the fridge. Keep the sauce separate and reheat the tofu in the air fryer at 375°F for 4 min to re-crisp.

## Instant Pot Red Lentil Coconut Curry

**Serves:** 4 (2 dinners for two) · **Time:** 30 min (hands-on 10) · **Equipment:** Instant Pot · **Est. cost:** ~$12 total (~$3/serving)

| Per serving | Amount | % of 1,600 day | % of 2,000 day |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Calories | 530 kcal | 33% | 26% |
| Protein | 37 g | 31% | 25% |
| Carbs | 60 g | 38% | 30% |
| Fat | 16 g | 30% | 24% |
| Fiber | 16 g | 42% | 42% |
| Sodium | 620 mg | 27% | 27% |

**Storage:** 🧊 Freezer-friendly (add the yogurt when serving)

**Why it's filling:** a thick, creamy dal-style curry that's a full meal in a bowl with no rice needed. Tofu and a yogurt swirl push protein to about 37 g.

### Ingredients
- 1½ cups dry red lentils, rinsed
- 1 can (13.5 oz) light coconut milk
- 1 can (14.5 oz) diced tomatoes
- 1 onion (diced), 4 cloves garlic, 2 Tbsp grated ginger
- 2 Tbsp curry powder (or 1 Tbsp curry powder + 1 tsp turmeric + 1 tsp cumin)
- 1 tsp oil, ½ tsp salt
- 3 cups low-sodium vegetable or chicken broth
- 1 block (14 oz) extra-firm tofu, cut into ½" cubes
- 5 oz baby spinach
- 1 cup nonfat Greek yogurt (¼ cup per bowl), lime and cilantro to finish

### Instructions
1. Instant Pot on **Sauté** with the oil: cook the onion 3 minutes. Add the garlic, ginger and curry powder and stir for 1 minute.
2. Add the broth and scrape the bottom. Add the lentils, tofu and salt, then pour the tomatoes and coconut milk on top without stirring.
3. Seal and **Pressure Cook High 6 min**, then natural release for 10 minutes.
4. Stir in the spinach until wilted. Serve each bowl with ¼ cup of Greek yogurt and a squeeze of lime.

### Notes
- Want rice? ½ cup cooked basmati adds about 100 kcal, or use cauliflower rice for about 25 kcal.
- Swap: 1½ cups shelled edamame instead of tofu (more fiber, but about 4 g less protein per bowl).
- Storage: 4 days in the fridge or 3 months frozen. It thickens as it sits, so loosen it with water when reheating.
- Freezing: freeze it without the spinach wilted in if you can (add fresh spinach when reheating), in 2-serving containers. Thaw overnight in the fridge or microwave from frozen, covered, stirring every 2 minutes. Add the yogurt only after reheating; it separates if frozen.
