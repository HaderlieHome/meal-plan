# Protein Powder Breakfasts

Blender shakes for the laziest mornings, plus a freezer pancake batch for the weekends. Assumes a typical whey protein (1 scoop ≈ 30 g, 120 kcal, 24 g protein).

## PB Banana Protein Shake

**Serves:** 2 · **Time:** 5 min (hands-on 5) · **Equipment:** Ninja blender · **Est. cost:** $4.00 total

| Per serving | Amount | % of 1,600 day | % of 2,000 day |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Calories | 321 kcal | 20% | 16% |
| Protein | 34 g | 28% | 23% |
| Carbs | 29 g | 18% | 14% |
| Fat | 9 g | 17% | 13% |
| Fiber | 8 g | 21% | 21% |
| Sodium | 370 mg | 16% | 16% |

**Storage:** 🔥 Make fresh — 5 min (pre-portion the dry ingredients in bags)

**Why it's filling:** powdered peanut butter gives the full PB flavor at a quarter of the fat, and chia thickens the shake so it drinks like a milkshake.

### Ingredients
- 2 cups unsweetened almond milk
- 2 scoops chocolate or vanilla whey protein
- 4 tbsp powdered peanut butter (e.g. PB2)
- 1 frozen banana
- 2 tbsp chia seeds
- 1 cup ice

### Instructions
1. Add the liquid first, then the powders, then the banana, chia and ice.
2. Blend on high 45–60 sec until thick and smooth. Split between two glasses.

### Notes
- **More protein:** use ultra-filtered fat-free milk (Fairlife) instead of almond milk for about 45 g protein and 370 kcal per serving.
- **Meal prep:** freeze peeled banana halves and pre-portion the dry ingredients into bags, so each morning is dump and blend.
- **Storage:** best fresh. It keeps 24 hours in the fridge; shake before drinking.

## Matcha Vanilla Protein Shake

**Serves:** 2 · **Time:** 5 min (hands-on 5) · **Equipment:** Ninja blender · **Est. cost:** $5.35 total

| Per serving | Amount | % of 1,600 day | % of 2,000 day |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Calories | 329 kcal | 21% | 16% |
| Protein | 40 g | 33% | 27% |
| Carbs | 32 g | 20% | 16% |
| Fat | 5 g | 9% | 7% |
| Fiber | 8 g | 21% | 21% |
| Sodium | 270 mg | 12% | 12% |

**Storage:** 🔥 Make fresh — 5 min

**Why it's filling:** frozen cauliflower rice (you can't taste it) makes it thick and frosty, adding volume and fiber for almost no calories. Matcha gives calm, steady energy from caffeine plus L-theanine.

### Ingredients
- 2 cups ultra-filtered fat-free milk (e.g. Fairlife)
- 2 scoops vanilla whey protein
- 2 tsp culinary-grade matcha powder
- 1 frozen banana
- 2 tbsp chia seeds
- 1 cup frozen riced cauliflower
- 1 cup ice

### Instructions
1. Add the milk, protein and matcha to the blender. Pulse to mix so the matcha doesn't clump.
2. Add the banana, chia, cauliflower and ice. Blend on high 60 sec.

### Notes
- **Swaps:** frozen mango instead of banana for a tropical version. Use almond milk if you want fewer calories (protein drops to ~29 g).
- **Storage:** best fresh.

## Protein Pancakes (Freezer Batch)

**Serves:** 4 (about 12 pancakes, 3 per serving, with toppings) · **Time:** 25 min (hands-on 20) · **Equipment:** Ninja blender, griddle/skillet, air fryer or toaster to reheat · **Est. cost:** $7.20 total

| Per serving | Amount | % of 1,600 day | % of 2,000 day |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Calories | 337 kcal | 21% | 17% |
| Protein | 36 g | 30% | 24% |
| Carbs | 39 g | 24% | 20% |
| Fat | 5 g | 9% | 7% |
| Fiber | 6 g | 16% | 16% |
| Sodium | 340 mg | 15% | 15% |

**Storage:** 🧊 Freezer-friendly — 3 months

**Why it's filling:** blended oats and Greek yogurt make fluffy, dense pancakes, and the yogurt-and-berry topping replaces butter and syrup. *Egg check:* 1 egg as a binder across 12 pancakes, so it's allowed.

### Ingredients
**Pancakes**
- 1 cup rolled oats
- 2 scoops vanilla whey protein
- 1 cup nonfat plain Greek yogurt
- 1 large egg
- ½ cup ultra-filtered fat-free milk
- 1 ripe banana
- 2 tbsp ground flaxseed
- 1 tsp baking powder, 1 tsp cinnamon, ⅛ tsp salt
- Cooking spray

**Toppings (per serving)**
- ½ cup nonfat Greek yogurt
- ½ cup warmed frozen berries (a quick jam)

### Instructions
1. Blend everything for the pancakes on high 30–45 sec until smooth. Let it rest 5 min to thicken.
2. Heat a sprayed nonstick skillet or griddle over medium. Pour ¼-cup rounds and cook ~2 min until bubbles form, flip, then cook 1 more min. Protein pancakes burn fast, so keep the heat at medium.
3. Serve with yogurt and berries, or cool completely for the freezer.

### Notes
- **Freezer:** lay the pancakes flat on a sheet pan and freeze 1 hr, then bag them. They keep 3 months.
- **Reheat:** air fryer at 350°F for 3–4 min (crisp edges), or a toaster from frozen.
- **Double it:** the Ninja blender handles a double batch easily, giving 8 servings.
