## Greek Yogurt & Granola Bowl

**Serves:** 2 · **Time:** 2 min · **Equipment:** none · **Est. cost:** ~$4

| Per serving | Amount | % of 1,600 day | % of 2,000 day |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Calories | 360 kcal | 22% | 18% |
| Protein | 34.5 g | 29% | 23% |
| Carbs | 44.5 g | 28% | 22% |
| Fat | 5 g | 9% | 7% |
| Fiber | 3.5 g | 9% | 9% |
| Sodium | 176 mg | 8% | 8% |

**Storage:** 🔥 Make fresh. It takes 2 minutes.

**Why it's filling:** A big 1½-cup bowl of Greek yogurt is over 30 g of protein.

### Ingredients (for two)
- 3 cups plain Greek yogurt
- ½ cup granola
- 1 cup frozen mixed berries (or fresh)
- 2 tsp honey

### Instructions
1. Split the yogurt between two bowls and stir in the honey.
2. Dump the berries and granola on top.

### Notes
- Frozen berries thaw in a few minutes, or microwave them 30 sec.

## Greek Yogurt Overnight Oats

**Serves:** 4 jars (2 mornings for two) · **Time:** 5 min + overnight · **Equipment:** none · **Est. cost:** ~$6

| Per serving | Amount | % of 1,600 day | % of 2,000 day |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Calories | 378 kcal | 24% | 19% |
| Protein | 32.5 g | 27% | 22% |
| Carbs | 52.5 g | 33% | 26% |
| Fat | 5 g | 9% | 7% |
| Fiber | 4 g | 11% | 11% |
| Sodium | 180 mg | 8% | 8% |

**Storage:** 🥡 Fridge prep — 4 days.

**Why it's filling:** Oats plus a cup of Greek yogurt per jar keeps you full until lunch.

### Ingredients (4 jars)
- 2 cups old-fashioned oats
- 4 cups plain Greek yogurt
- 3 cups milk
- 2 tbsp honey

### Instructions
1. Stir everything together in a big bowl.
2. Split into 4 jars or containers.
3. Fridge overnight. Eat cold or microwave 1–2 min.

## Cottage Cheese, Banana & Toast

**Serves:** 2 · **Time:** 3 min · **Equipment:** toaster · **Est. cost:** ~$4

| Per serving | Amount | % of 1,600 day | % of 2,000 day |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Calories | 404 kcal | 25% | 20% |
| Protein | 34 g | 28% | 23% |
| Carbs | 52.5 g | 33% | 26% |
| Fat | 8 g | 15% | 12% |
| Fiber | 5 g | 13% | 13% |
| Sodium | 1,001 mg | 44% | 44% |

**Storage:** 🔥 Make fresh. It takes 3 minutes.

**Why it's filling:** 1¼ cups of cottage cheese is about 30 g of slow-digesting protein.

### Ingredients (for two)
- 2½ cups cottage cheese
- 2 slices whole wheat bread
- 2 bananas

### Instructions
1. Toast the bread.
2. Scoop the cottage cheese into two bowls and slice a banana on top of each.
