## Greek Yogurt Berry Parfait

**Serves:** 2 · **Time:** 5 min (hands-on 5) · **Equipment:** none · **Est. cost:** $4.75 total

| Per serving | Amount | % of 1,600 day | % of 2,000 day |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Calories | 353 kcal | 22% | 18% |
| Protein | 38 g | 32% | 25% |
| Carbs | 49 g | 31% | 24% |
| Fat | 6 g | 11% | 9% |
| Fiber | 18 g | 47% | 47% |
| Sodium | 210 mg | 9% | 9% |

**Storage:** 🥡 Fridge prep — 4 days (keep the cereal separate so it stays crunchy)

**Why it's filling:** a full cup of Greek yogurt plus 18 g of fiber from the cereal, chia and berries, so it's a big bowl that holds you until lunch.

### Ingredients
- 2 cups nonfat plain Greek yogurt
- 1 scoop vanilla whey protein (split between the bowls; sweetens the yogurt, so no honey is needed)
- 2 tbsp chia seeds
- ⅔ cup high-fiber cereal (e.g. Fiber One Original)
- 2 cups frozen mixed berries (thawed overnight in the fridge, or microwaved 60 sec)

### Instructions
1. Stir the protein powder into the yogurt until smooth. Add a splash of water or milk if it's too thick.
2. Layer each bowl: yogurt, berries (with their juice), chia, then cereal on top.
3. Eat right away so the cereal stays crunchy.

### Notes
- **Meal prep:** portion the yogurt, protein and chia into 4–5 containers on Sunday, with the berries on top. Keep the cereal separate and add it in the morning.
- **Mental health bonus:** yogurt cultures (gut), berries (antioxidants), chia (omega-3 ALA).
- **Storage:** the yogurt, protein and berry base keeps 4 days in the fridge.

## Protein Overnight Oats

**Serves:** 4 jars (2 mornings for two) · **Time:** 10 min (hands-on 10) + overnight · **Equipment:** none · **Est. cost:** $8.10 total

| Per serving | Amount | % of 1,600 day | % of 2,000 day |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Calories | 428 kcal | 27% | 21% |
| Protein | 40 g | 33% | 27% |
| Carbs | 52 g | 32% | 26% |
| Fat | 7 g | 13% | 10% |
| Fiber | 10 g | 26% | 26% |
| Sodium | 230 mg | 10% | 10% |

**Storage:** 🥡 Fridge prep — 4 days

**Why it's filling:** oats and chia swell overnight into a thick, dense jar, and the slow-digesting carbs keep blood sugar steady all morning.

### Ingredients
- 2 cups old-fashioned rolled oats
- 4 tbsp chia seeds
- 3 cups ultra-filtered fat-free milk (e.g. Fairlife)
- 2 cups nonfat plain Greek yogurt
- 2 scoops vanilla whey protein
- 2 cups frozen mixed berries (for topping)
- 1 tsp cinnamon, pinch of salt

### Instructions
1. In a large bowl, whisk the milk, yogurt and protein powder until smooth.
2. Stir in the oats, chia, cinnamon and salt.
3. Divide between 4 jars or containers and top each with ½ cup frozen berries (they thaw overnight).
4. Refrigerate at least 6 hours. Eat cold, or microwave 1–2 min for warm oats.

### Notes
- **Flavor swaps:** chocolate protein and 1 tbsp cocoa with banana; PB2 and banana; apple and extra cinnamon.
- **Storage:** 4 days in the fridge. It thickens as it sits, so add a splash of milk if needed.

## Savory Cottage Cheese Bowl

**Serves:** 2 · **Time:** 5 min (hands-on 5) · **Equipment:** toaster · **Est. cost:** $5.50 total

| Per serving | Amount | % of 1,600 day | % of 2,000 day |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Calories | 377 kcal | 24% | 19% |
| Protein | 33 g | 28% | 22% |
| Carbs | 39 g | 24% | 20% |
| Fat | 10 g | 19% | 15% |
| Fiber | 5 g | 13% | 13% |
| Sodium | 1090 mg | 47% | 47% |

**Storage:** 🥡 Fridge prep — 3 days (dice the veg ahead, assemble fresh)

**Why it's filling:** cottage cheese is mostly casein, a slow-digesting protein, and the pile of crunchy veg adds lots of volume for very few calories.

### Ingredients
- 2 cups 2% cottage cheese
- 1 English cucumber, diced (about 2 cups)
- 1 cup cherry tomatoes, halved
- 1 tsp everything bagel seasoning
- 2 slices whole-wheat bread, toasted
- 2 tbsp pumpkin seeds (pepitas)
- Optional: black pepper, a drizzle of chili crisp, fresh dill

### Instructions
1. Split the cottage cheese between two bowls.
2. Top with cucumber, tomatoes, pepitas and everything seasoning.
3. Eat with the toast on the side, or scoop it onto the toast.

### Notes
- **Sodium:** cottage cheese is salty. Look for a lower-sodium brand (some are around 350 mg per ½ cup), or go light on the seasoning.
- **Japanese twist:** swap the everything seasoning for furikake and add a few drops of sesame oil.
- **Storage:** dice the veg ahead; it keeps 3 days in the fridge.
