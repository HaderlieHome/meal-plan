## Turkey Sausage & Toast Plate

**Serves:** 2 · **Time:** 3 min · **Equipment:** microwave, toaster · **Est. cost:** ~$5

| Per serving | Amount | % of 1,600 day | % of 2,000 day |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Calories | 404 kcal | 25% | 20% |
| Protein | 38 g | 32% | 25% |
| Carbs | 34.5 g | 22% | 17% |
| Fat | 14 g | 27% | 21% |
| Fiber | 4 g | 11% | 11% |
| Sodium | 860 mg | 37% | 37% |

**Storage:** 🔥 Make fresh. It takes 3 minutes.

**Why it's filling:** Sausage plus a protein yogurt cup is close to 40 g of protein.

### Ingredients (for two)
- 6 fully cooked turkey sausage links (Bob Evans or Jimmy Dean)
- 4 slices whole wheat bread
- 2 tsp butter
- 2 Greek yogurt cups (Oikos Pro or similar)

### Instructions
1. Microwave the sausage links per the package (about 1 min).
2. Toast and butter the bread.
3. Eat with a yogurt cup.

## Canadian Bacon English Muffin Sandwich

**Serves:** 2 · **Time:** 3 min · **Equipment:** microwave, toaster · **Est. cost:** ~$5

| Per serving | Amount | % of 1,600 day | % of 2,000 day |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Calories | 405 kcal | 25% | 20% |
| Protein | 40 g | 33% | 27% |
| Carbs | 33.5 g | 21% | 17% |
| Fat | 12.5 g | 24% | 19% |
| Fiber | 3 g | 8% | 8% |
| Sodium | 892 mg | 39% | 39% |

**Storage:** 🔥 Make fresh. It takes 3 minutes.

**Why it's filling:** A hot breakfast sandwich plus a protein yogurt cup is about 40 g of protein.

### Ingredients (for two)
- 2 whole wheat English muffins
- 6 slices Canadian bacon
- 2 slices cheddar
- 2 Greek yogurt cups (Oikos Pro or similar)

### Instructions
1. Toast the English muffins.
2. Stack 3 slices Canadian bacon and a slice of cheese on each bottom half. Microwave 30 sec.
3. Put the top on. Eat with a yogurt cup.
