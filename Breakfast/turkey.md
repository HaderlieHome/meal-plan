# Turkey Breakfasts

## Freezer Breakfast Burritos (No Egg)

**Serves:** 10 burritos (1 per serving) · **Time:** 50 min (hands-on 30) · **Equipment:** air fryer, skillet · **Est. cost:** $22.00 total (~$2.20 each)

| Per serving | Amount | % of 1,600 day | % of 2,000 day |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Calories | 401 kcal | 25% | 20% |
| Protein | 33 g | 28% | 22% |
| Carbs | 51 g | 32% | 26% |
| Fat | 17 g | 32% | 25% |
| Fiber | 29 g | 76% | 76% |
| Sodium | 1130 mg | 49% | 49% |

**Storage:** 🧊 Freezer-friendly — 3 months

**Why it's filling:** high-fiber tortillas and black beans pack about 29 g of fiber into each burrito, and crispy potatoes deliver the starch without the calories of hash browns fried in oil.

### Ingredients
- 2 lb 93% lean ground turkey
- **Sausage spice:** 2 tsp dried sage, 1 tsp fennel seed (crushed), 1 tsp smoked paprika, 1 tsp garlic powder, ½ tsp black pepper, ¼ tsp red pepper flakes, ¾ tsp salt
- 1½ lb Yukon gold or russet potatoes, diced ½-inch (skin on)
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1 can (15 oz) black beans, drained and rinsed
- 1½ cups shredded reduced-fat Mexican cheese
- 1 cup salsa (thick/chunky, so the burritos don't get soggy)
- 10 high-fiber burrito-size tortillas (e.g. Mission Carb Balance)

### Instructions
1. **Potatoes:** toss the potatoes with the oil and a pinch of the spice mix. Air fry at 400°F for 18–20 min, shaking twice. Do it in two rounds if the basket is crowded.
2. **Turkey:** while the potatoes cook, brown the turkey with the sausage spice in a large skillet, 8–10 min, breaking it up, until no pink remains (165°F). Stir in the beans to warm.
3. **Cool:** spread the filling and potatoes on a sheet pan and let cool 10 min. Hot filling steams the tortillas and makes them soggy in the freezer.
4. **Assemble:** warm the tortillas 20 sec in the microwave so they roll without cracking. Fill each with about ⅔ cup turkey and beans, a scoop of potatoes, 2 tbsp cheese and a spoonful of salsa. Roll tight, folding in the sides.
5. **Wrap:** wrap each burrito in foil or plastic wrap, then put them all in a gallon freezer bag.

### Notes
- **Reheat (from frozen):** remove the foil, wrap in a damp paper towel and microwave 2 min, flipping halfway. Then air fry at 375°F for 3–4 min to crisp the outside.
- **Sodium:** the tortillas and salsa add up. Choose a low-sodium salsa to cut ~150 mg per burrito.
- **Storage:** 3 months in the freezer, 4 days in the fridge.

## Turkey Sausage & Sweet Potato Hash

**Serves:** 4 (2 mornings for two) · **Time:** 30 min (hands-on 15) · **Equipment:** air fryer, skillet · **Est. cost:** $13.00 total

| Per serving | Amount | % of 1,600 day | % of 2,000 day |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Calories | 418 kcal | 26% | 21% |
| Protein | 41 g | 34% | 27% |
| Carbs | 30 g | 19% | 15% |
| Fat | 13 g | 25% | 19% |
| Fiber | 6 g | 16% | 16% |
| Sodium | 780 mg | 34% | 34% |

**Storage:** 🥡 Fridge prep — 4 days (or 🧊 2 months without the yogurt)

**Why it's filling:** a big, colorful plate of sweet potato, peppers and spinach alongside the turkey, with a cool Greek yogurt side that adds 12 g of protein.

### Ingredients
- 1¼ lb 93% lean ground turkey
- Sausage spice (same as the burritos above; use ½ tsp salt in the turkey)
- 2 medium sweet potatoes (~14 oz), diced ½-inch
- 2 bell peppers, diced
- 1 onion, diced
- 4 cups baby spinach
- 2 tsp olive oil, ½ tsp salt
- **Side:** 2 cups nonfat plain Greek yogurt with hot sauce or everything seasoning

### Instructions
1. Toss the sweet potatoes, peppers and onion with the oil and ½ tsp salt. Air fry at 400°F for 15–18 min, shaking halfway. Do two rounds if needed.
2. Meanwhile, brown the turkey with the sausage spice in a skillet, 8–10 min, until no pink remains (165°F).
3. Add the spinach to the skillet and stir until wilted, 1 min. Fold in the air-fried veg.
4. Serve each portion with ½ cup yogurt on the side, or dolloped on top like sour cream.

### Notes
- **Meal prep:** portion into 4 containers, keeping the yogurt separate.
- **Reheat:** air fryer at 375°F for 5 min brings back the crispy edges; the microwave works in 2 min.
- **Storage:** 4 days in the fridge; freeze up to 2 months (without the yogurt).
