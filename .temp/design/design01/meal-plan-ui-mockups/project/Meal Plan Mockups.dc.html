Sheet-Pan Chicken Fajitas

Dinner · 30 min · makes 4 servings

Per serving
Protein48 g
Calories460
Carbs34 g
Fat14 g

Ingredients

  • 2 lb chicken breast, sliced thin
  • 3 bell peppers, sliced
  • 1 red onion, sliced
  • 2 tbsp fajita seasoning
  • 8 low-carb tortillas
  • Plain Greek yogurt, for topping

Steps

  1. Heat oven to 425°F. Line a sheet pan.
  2. Toss chicken, peppers and onion with oil and seasoning. Spread in one layer.
  3. Roast 20–22 minutes, stirring once.
  4. Split into 4 containers. Warm tortillas when serving.
Prep tip. Greek yogurt stands in for sour cream and adds about 6 g protein.

About the plan

Recipes here are written for two people who want to eat well without cooking every night. Most dinners make four servings, so one cook covers two days.

Every recipe lists calories and protein per serving so the planner can add up each day.

Rules of thumb

  • At least 35 g protein per meal.
  • One pan or one pot whenever possible.
  • Cook twice a week, eat leftovers the rest.
