# Seafood Lunches

These use shelf-stable pouch tuna and salmon. **Open the pouch and mix it at lunch, never before.** Nothing seafood gets pre-mixed or stored, following the README's seafood rule.

**Leftover dinner counts, except seafood.** Seafood dinners are always cook fresh, so they never become leftovers.

## Pouch Tuna Salad Sandwich

**Serves:** 2 (1 lunch for two) · **Time:** 5 min at lunch (hands-on 5) · **Equipment:** none · **Est. cost:** ~$10 total

| Per serving | Amount | % of 1,600 day | % of 2,000 day |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Calories | 508 kcal | 32% | 25% |
| Protein | 46 g | 38% | 31% |
| Carbs | 73 g | 46% | 36% |
| Fat | 8 g | 15% | 12% |
| Fiber | 12 g | 32% | 32% |
| Sodium | 1,070 mg | 47% | 47% |

**Storage:** 🔥 Mix the same day. Pack the sealed pouches with the bread and a small container of the yogurt dressing, then mix at lunch.

**Why it's filling:** two whole pouches of tuna per sandwich make it very high in protein for the calories, and the apple and chips round it out.

### Ingredients
- 4 slices Dave's Killer Bread Thin-Sliced 21 Whole Grains
- 4 pouches (2.6 oz each) StarKist Chunk Light Tuna in Water
- ¼ cup plain nonfat Greek yogurt
- 2 tsp light mayo
- 2 tsp Dijon or yellow mustard
- 2 romaine leaves
- 2 medium apples
- 2 single-serve bags (1 oz each) Lay's Baked chips
- Optional: relish, black pepper, lemon juice

### Instructions
1. **The night before:** stir the Greek yogurt, mayo and mustard together in 2 small lidded containers, and refrigerate them.
2. **Pack:** 2 sealed tuna pouches, 2 slices of bread, a lettuce leaf, the dressing cup, an apple and a bag of chips per person. Keep it cold with an ice pack or the office fridge.
3. **At lunch:** tear open the pouches, stir the tuna into the dressing cup, and spread it on the bread with the lettuce.

### Notes
- If you don't have a fridge at work, use an ice pack for the yogurt cup. The tuna pouches are shelf-stable until opened.
- Protein is a little over the 45 g target. Use 1½ pouches each if you'd rather hit 40 g.

## Spicy Salmon Wrap

**Serves:** 2 (1 lunch for two) · **Time:** 5 min at lunch (hands-on 5) · **Equipment:** none · **Est. cost:** ~$13 total

| Per serving | Amount | % of 1,600 day | % of 2,000 day |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Calories | 500 kcal | 31% | 25% |
| Protein | 48 g | 40% | 32% |
| Carbs | 59 g | 37% | 30% |
| Fat | 20 g | 38% | 30% |
| Fiber | 30 g | 79% | 79% |
| Sodium | 1,150 mg | 50% | 50% |

**Storage:** 🔥 Mix the same day. Pack the sealed pouches and assemble the wrap at lunch.

**Why it's filling:** salmon's protein and omega-3 fat plus a high-fiber wrap, crunchy cucumber and popcorn on the side.

### Ingredients
- 2 Mission Carb Balance whole wheat tortillas
- 4 pouches (2.6 oz each) pink salmon (e.g. StarKist or Chicken of the Sea)
- ¼ cup plain nonfat Greek yogurt + 1–2 tsp sriracha (a spicy "mayo")
- ½ cucumber, cut into matchsticks
- 1 cup baby spinach
- 4 clementines
- 1 oz SkinnyPop popcorn (½ oz each)

### Instructions
1. **The night before:** mix the yogurt and sriracha in 2 small lidded containers. Bag the cucumber and spinach.
2. **Pack:** 2 sealed pouches, a tortilla, the sauce cup, the veg bag, 2 clementines and popcorn per person.
3. **At lunch:** open the salmon, stir it into the sauce, and roll it up in the tortilla with the cucumber and spinach.

### Notes
- Omega-3s from salmon support brain health (see the README's mental-health section).
- For a Japanese nod, add a sprinkle of furikake or sesame seeds and a squeeze of lime.
