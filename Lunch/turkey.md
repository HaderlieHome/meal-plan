# Turkey Lunches

Classic deli-turkey sandwiches with a real side. They take about 5 minutes to build and need no cooking.

**Leftover dinner counts.** Leftover chili, sloppy joes or gochujang turkey bowls make a lunch too.

- [Turkey Club + Grapes + Popcorn](#turkey-club--grapes--popcorn) — 544 kcal · 37 g protein
- [Japanese-Style Turkey Cucumber Sando](#japanese-style-turkey-cucumber-sando) — 495 kcal · 45 g protein

## Turkey Club + Grapes + Popcorn

**Serves:** 2 (1 lunch for two) · **Time:** 7 min (hands-on 7) · **Equipment:** none (toaster optional) · **Est. cost:** ~$10 total

| Per serving | Amount | % of 1,600 day | % of 2,000 day |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Calories | 544 kcal | 34% | 27% |
| Protein | 37 g | 31% | 25% |
| Carbs | 65 g | 41% | 33% |
| Fat | 17 g | 32% | 25% |
| Fiber | 10 g | 26% | 26% |
| Sodium | 1,250 mg | 54% | 54% |

**Storage:** 🥡 Fridge prep. Build it the night before or the morning of, and keep the tomato in a separate bag so the bread doesn't get soggy.

**Why it's filling:** a full sandwich with bacon, a pile of grapes and a big bag of popcorn feels like a lot of lunch.

### Ingredients
- 4 slices Dave's Killer Bread Thin-Sliced 21 Whole Grains
- 7 oz lower-sodium oven-roasted deli turkey
- 2 slices fully cooked bacon (e.g. Oscar Mayer)
- 1 Tbsp light mayo
- 1 small tomato, sliced
- 2 romaine leaves
- 2 light mozzarella string cheese sticks
- 1¾ cups red grapes
- 1 oz SkinnyPop popcorn (½ oz, about 1¾ cups, each)

### Instructions
1. Spread the mayo on the bread. Layer the turkey, bacon (warm it 15 seconds in the microwave if you like it crisper), lettuce and tomato.
2. Cut the sandwiches and wrap them.
3. Pack each with a string cheese, grapes and a bag of popcorn.

### Notes
- Deli turkey is most of the sodium, so buy the lowest-sodium one you can find (under 450 mg per 2 oz).
- Skip the bacon to save 30 kcal and 140 mg sodium per sandwich.

## Japanese-Style Turkey Cucumber Sando

**Serves:** 2 (1 lunch for two) · **Time:** 7 min (hands-on 7) · **Equipment:** none · **Est. cost:** ~$10 total

| Per serving | Amount | % of 1,600 day | % of 2,000 day |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Calories | 495 kcal | 31% | 25% |
| Protein | 45 g | 38% | 30% |
| Carbs | 67 g | 42% | 34% |
| Fat | 7 g | 13% | 10% |
| Fiber | 12 g | 32% | 32% |
| Sodium | 1,050 mg | 46% | 46% |

**Storage:** 🥡 Fridge prep. Make it the night before and wrap it tight in plastic wrap. It's meant to be eaten cold.

**Why it's filling:** a stacked, crusts-off sando with a vanilla Greek yogurt cup and a whole pear.

### Ingredients
- 4 slices Dave's Killer Bread Thin-Sliced 21 Whole Grains
- 8½ oz lower-sodium oven-roasted deli turkey
- 1 Tbsp light mayo + 2 Tbsp plain nonfat Greek yogurt + ½ tsp rice vinegar (a light stand-in for Kewpie mayo)
- ½ cucumber, sliced very thin
- 2 Oikos Triple Zero vanilla Greek yogurt cups (5.3 oz each)
- 2 pears
- Optional: a few drops of sriracha or a pinch of sugar in the mayo mix

### Instructions
1. Stir the mayo, Greek yogurt and rice vinegar together, then spread it on all 4 slices of bread.
2. Layer the turkey and cucumber, press the sandwich closed, trim the crusts if you like, and cut in half.
3. Wrap tight. Pack each with a yogurt cup and a pear.

### Notes
- Blot the cucumber slices with a paper towel before layering so the bread stays firm.
- Leftover dinner chicken katsu, sliced cold, also makes a great sando in this style.
