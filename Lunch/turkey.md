## Turkey & Cheese Sandwich + Chips

**Serves:** 2 · **Time:** 3 min · **Equipment:** none · **Est. cost:** ~$8

| Per serving | Amount | % of 1,600 day | % of 2,000 day |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Calories | 515 kcal | 32% | 26% |
| Protein | 37 g | 31% | 25% |
| Carbs | 43.5 g | 27% | 22% |
| Fat | 19.5 g | 37% | 29% |
| Fiber | 5.5 g | 14% | 14% |
| Sodium | 1,526 mg | 66% | 66% |

**Storage:** 🥡 Fridge — make it the night before or the morning of.

**Why it's filling:** A thick stack of 5 oz of turkey per sandwich.

### Ingredients (for two)
- 4 slices whole wheat bread
- 10 oz deli turkey (lower-sodium)
- 2 slices provolone or American cheese
- Mustard
- 2 snack bags (1 oz) potato chips

### Instructions
1. Stack turkey and cheese on the bread with mustard.
2. Pack with a bag of chips.

### Notes
- Swap the chips for an apple to save about 65 kcal.
