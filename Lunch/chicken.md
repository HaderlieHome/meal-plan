## Rotisserie Chicken Caesar Wrap

**Serves:** 2 · **Time:** 5 min · **Equipment:** none · **Est. cost:** ~$9

| Per serving | Amount | % of 1,600 day | % of 2,000 day |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Calories | 540 kcal | 34% | 27% |
| Protein | 45 g | 37% | 30% |
| Carbs | 50.5 g | 32% | 25% |
| Fat | 17 g | 32% | 25% |
| Fiber | 3.5 g | 9% | 9% |
| Sodium | 1,138 mg | 49% | 49% |

**Storage:** 🥡 Fridge — build it the night before or the morning of.

**Why it's filling:** A big wrap stuffed with a quarter pound of chicken.

### Ingredients (for two)
- 2 large flour tortillas (burrito size)
- 8 oz rotisserie chicken breast, pulled
- About ⅖ of a bagged Caesar salad kit (with its dressing, croutons and cheese)
- 2 clementines

### Instructions
1. Toss the salad kit with its dressing.
2. Pile chicken and salad down the middle of each tortilla and roll it up.
3. Pack with a clementine.

### Notes
- Save the rest of the salad kit for tomorrow's wraps.

## Buffalo Chicken Wrap

**Serves:** 2 · **Time:** 5 min · **Equipment:** none · **Est. cost:** ~$8

| Per serving | Amount | % of 1,600 day | % of 2,000 day |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Calories | 501 kcal | 31% | 25% |
| Protein | 42 g | 35% | 28% |
| Carbs | 45 g | 28% | 22% |
| Fat | 15.5 g | 29% | 23% |
| Fiber | 4.5 g | 12% | 12% |
| Sodium | 1,426 mg | 62% | 62% |

**Storage:** 🥡 Fridge — build it the night before or the morning of.

**Why it's filling:** A quarter pound of spicy chicken in a big wrap, with carrots for crunch.

### Ingredients (for two)
- 2 large flour tortillas (burrito size)
- 8 oz rotisserie chicken breast, pulled
- 4 tsp Frank's RedHot
- 2 tbsp ranch
- 1 cup shredded lettuce
- About 20 baby carrots

### Instructions
1. Toss the chicken with the hot sauce.
2. Lay the lettuce and chicken on each tortilla, drizzle ranch, and roll it up.
3. Pack with the carrots.
