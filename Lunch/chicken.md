# Chicken Lunches

Simple wraps and boxes built on Costco rotisserie chicken. You assemble them, with no cooking. Pull the meat off one chicken on Sunday and it covers several lunches.

**Leftover dinner counts.** Any leftover chicken dinner in a container is a lunch.

## Rotisserie Chicken Caesar Wrap

**Serves:** 2 (1 lunch for two) · **Time:** 8 min (hands-on 8) · **Equipment:** none · **Est. cost:** ~$9 total

| Per serving | Amount | % of 1,600 day | % of 2,000 day |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Calories | 544 kcal | 34% | 27% |
| Protein | 41 g | 34% | 27% |
| Carbs | 81 g | 51% | 40% |
| Fat | 19 g | 36% | 28% |
| Fiber | 31 g | 82% | 82% |
| Sodium | 1,200 mg | 52% | 52% |

**Storage:** 🥡 Fridge prep. The pulled chicken keeps 3–4 days. Wrap the night before or the morning of.

**Why it's filling:** a high-fiber tortilla and a whole apple give real chewing volume, and the chicken carries the protein.

### Ingredients
- 2 Mission Carb Balance whole wheat tortillas (burrito size)
- 8 oz (about 1½ cups) rotisserie chicken breast, pulled
- 2 cups chopped romaine (or ½ bag Caesar salad kit, croutons skipped)
- 2 Tbsp grated parmesan
- 3 Tbsp light Caesar dressing (e.g. Wish-Bone Light)
- 2 medium apples
- 2 single-serve bags (1 oz each) Lay's Baked chips

### Instructions
1. Toss the romaine, chicken, parmesan and dressing in a bowl.
2. Split the mix between the tortillas, roll them tight and cut in half.
3. Wrap in foil or put in a container. Pack with an apple and a bag of chips.

### Notes
- The high-fiber tortilla is most of the fiber, and its label calories (110) are accurate even though the carbs look high. Most of those carbs are fiber.
- To lower the sodium, use half the dressing plus a squeeze of lemon, or choose a lower-sodium rotisserie chicken. Costco's is salty, so check the label.
- Storage: pack the dressing separately if you're wrapping the night before, so the wrap doesn't get soggy.

## Buffalo Chicken Ranch Wrap

**Serves:** 2 (1 lunch for two) · **Time:** 8 min (hands-on 8) · **Equipment:** none · **Est. cost:** ~$8 total

| Per serving | Amount | % of 1,600 day | % of 2,000 day |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Calories | 428 kcal | 27% | 21% |
| Protein | 45 g | 38% | 30% |
| Carbs | 62 g | 39% | 31% |
| Fat | 12 g | 23% | 18% |
| Fiber | 31 g | 82% | 82% |
| Sodium | 1,400 mg | 61% | 61% |

**Storage:** 🥡 Fridge prep. The chicken keeps 3–4 days. Wrap the night before or the morning of.

**Why it's filling:** a big protein wrap with crunchy carrots and two clementines for volume, all under 450 kcal.

### Ingredients
- 2 Mission Carb Balance whole wheat tortillas
- 8½ oz (about 1¾ cups) rotisserie chicken breast, pulled
- 4 tsp Frank's RedHot
- 2 Tbsp light ranch dressing
- ⅓ cup plain nonfat Greek yogurt
- 2 cups shredded romaine
- 7 oz baby carrots (about 1½ cups)
- 4 clementines

### Instructions
1. Stir the Frank's into the chicken.
2. Mix the ranch and Greek yogurt together, spread it on the tortillas, then add the romaine and buffalo chicken.
3. Roll, cut and wrap. Pack with carrots and 2 clementines each.

### Notes
- Frank's is almost all of the extra sodium. Use 2 tsp for about 190 mg less per person.
- For more crunch, add sliced celery.

## Teriyaki Chicken Lettuce Wrap Box

**Serves:** 2 (1 lunch for two) · **Time:** 10 min (hands-on 10) · **Equipment:** none (microwave optional) · **Est. cost:** ~$9 total

| Per serving | Amount | % of 1,600 day | % of 2,000 day |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Calories | 427 kcal | 27% | 21% |
| Protein | 44 g | 37% | 29% |
| Carbs | 44 g | 28% | 22% |
| Fat | 10 g | 19% | 15% |
| Fiber | 8 g | 21% | 21% |
| Sodium | 890 mg | 39% | 39% |

**Storage:** 🥡 Fridge prep. Pack it the night before and keep the chicken and lettuce in separate compartments.

**Why it's filling:** a Japanese-style bento with edamame, cucumber and rice crackers that has a lot to eat for the calories.

### Ingredients
- 8 oz (about 1½ cups) rotisserie chicken breast, pulled
- 2 Tbsp teriyaki sauce
- 8 large romaine or butter lettuce leaves
- 1 small cucumber, sliced into spears
- 1¾ oz (about 1 cup) rice crackers
- 1⅓ cups frozen shelled edamame (it comes pre-cooked, so thaw it overnight in the fridge)
- 2 clementines
- Optional: sesame seeds, sliced green onion, sriracha

### Instructions
1. Toss the chicken with the teriyaki sauce. Eat it cold, or microwave it 45–60 seconds at lunch.
2. Pack each box with lettuce leaves, cucumber, edamame, rice crackers and a clementine.
3. At lunch, spoon the chicken into the lettuce cups and eat them like tacos.

### Notes
- Frozen shelled edamame is blanched before freezing, so it's safe to eat thawed without cooking.
- For lower sodium, use a reduced-sodium teriyaki.
