# Plant-Based Lunches

Meatless lunches that still hit the protein targets, using Greek yogurt, edamame and a protein shake. Assembly only.

## Hummus & Feta Veggie Pita Box

**Serves:** 2 (1 lunch for two) · **Time:** 8 min (hands-on 8) · **Equipment:** none · **Est. cost:** ~$10 total

| Per serving | Amount | % of 1,600 day | % of 2,000 day |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Calories | 539 kcal | 34% | 27% |
| Protein | 37 g | 31% | 25% |
| Carbs | 68 g | 42% | 34% |
| Fat | 16 g | 30% | 24% |
| Fiber | 14 g | 37% | 37% |
| Sodium | 710 mg | 31% | 31% |

**Storage:** 🥡 Fridge prep. Pack it the night before, but stuff the pita at lunch so it doesn't get soggy.

**Why it's filling:** a stuffed pita, a cup of edamame, 2 cups of strawberries and a yogurt cup. There's a lot of food and fiber for the calories.

### Ingredients
- 2 whole wheat pitas (6-inch)
- ⅓ cup hummus
- ⅓ cup crumbled feta
- ¾ cup sliced cucumber
- 1 small tomato, diced
- 1⅓ cups baby spinach
- 1 cup frozen shelled edamame, thawed overnight in the fridge (it comes pre-cooked)
- 2 Oikos Triple Zero vanilla Greek yogurt cups (5.3 oz each)
- 2 cups strawberries
- Optional: a squeeze of lemon, red pepper flakes, za'atar

### Instructions
1. Pack each box with a pita, a small cup of hummus, and the feta, cucumber, tomato and spinach mixed in a container.
2. Add a container of edamame (sprinkle it with a pinch of salt), a yogurt cup and the strawberries.
3. At lunch, spread hummus inside the pita and stuff it with the veg and feta.

### Notes
- To boost protein, add ½ cup rinsed canned chickpeas to the pita (+6 g protein, +100 kcal).
- The edamame is the protein hero here. Don't skip it.

## Grown-Up PB Banana Sandwich + Protein Shake

**Serves:** 2 (1 lunch for two) · **Time:** 4 min (hands-on 4) · **Equipment:** none · **Est. cost:** ~$9 total

| Per serving | Amount | % of 1,600 day | % of 2,000 day |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Calories | 529 kcal | 33% | 26% |
| Protein | 41 g | 34% | 27% |
| Carbs | 69 g | 43% | 34% |
| Fat | 15 g | 28% | 22% |
| Fiber | 14 g | 37% | 37% |
| Sodium | 520 mg | 23% | 23% |

**Storage:** 🥡 Fridge prep. The sandwich holds fine overnight. Chill the shakes.

**Why it's filling:** the comfort of a PB sandwich, with a ready-to-drink shake carrying most of the protein, plus a whole apple.

### Ingredients
- 4 slices Dave's Killer Bread Thin-Sliced 21 Whole Grains
- 2½ Tbsp peanut butter (about 1¼ Tbsp per sandwich)
- 1 small banana, sliced
- 2 bottles Fairlife Nutrition Plan protein shake (11.5 oz each, 30 g protein)
- 2 medium apples
- Optional: a sprinkle of cinnamon or a drizzle of honey

### Instructions
1. Spread the peanut butter on the bread and layer the banana slices.
2. Close, cut and wrap.
3. Pack each with a cold shake and an apple.

### Notes
- The shake nutrition is from the Fairlife label (150 kcal, 30 g protein, 4 g carbs, 2.5 g fat, 230 mg sodium), because it isn't in the OpenNutrition database. Other 30 g shakes, like Premier Protein, work the same way.
- Measure the peanut butter. It's the easiest place for extra calories to sneak in.
